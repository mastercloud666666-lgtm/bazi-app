2026 Detailed Horoscope for Those Born in the Year of the Rooster: Navigate the Bingwu Fire Year with Low-Key Stability

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-24 · Bazi Guide

The year 2026 is the Bingwu year, with Bing Fire in the Heavenly Stem and Wu Fire in the Earthly Branch. Overall, the energy of fire is strong, affecting different zodiac signs in completely different ways. For those born in the Year of the Rooster (Earthly Branch You, element Metal), their fortune in the Bingwu year needs to be analyzed based on the relationship between You and Wu.

Overall Fortune for Those Born in the Year of the Rooster in 2026

In 2026, the Bingwu year, Rooster (You) belongs to the Metal element, which is equally restrained by the Bingwu Fire. However, compared to Monkey (Shen Metal), You Metal is more steady and introverted, and is relatively less impacted. The overall fortune of those born in the year of the Rooster in 2026 is slightly below average. It is not advisable to make major moves; the main approach should be low-key actions and steady management, patiently waiting for the arrival of the next peak in fortune.

Core Points for the Year 2026, Bingwu: The heavenly stem Bing fire is revealed, and the earthly branch Wu fire is strong. The overall social environment is full of competition and vitality; those who follow the trend will benefit, while those who go against it will face pressure.

Career and Wealth Luck

In the workplace, it is advised to keep a low profile, avoid showing off or making enemies proactively. Focus on completing your own work diligently and reduce unnecessary competition. Financially, adopt a conservative approach, lower investment proportions, and increase savings. For those with a fixed salary income, stability can actually be an advantage this year.

Wealth Fortune Advice

  • Main source of wealth: Maintain a stable job income and avoid large speculative investments
  • Windfall Opportunities: Pay attention to investment opportunities in industries related to fire and wood
  • Note: Financial opportunities that arise this year need to be carefully identified, and be wary of verbal promises.

Emotions and Romantic Relationships

Emotional fortune is average. For single people, potential relationships are ordinary, but quality is more important than quantity. The person you meet this year requires time to get to know deeply before making a decision. Those already in a relationship should maintain the status quo and not pressure their partner into making too many commitments.

Relationship advice

  • Singles: Actively create social situations, opportunities for introductions by influential people are more frequent
  • Already has a partner: Focus on the quality of communication and avoid neglecting the relationship due to being busy with work.
  • Married individuals: Pay attention to your spouse's health and plan family goals together

Health Notice

Similar to those born in the Year of the Monkey, the lungs and respiratory system are health focus areas. In addition, You corresponds to the large intestine, so the digestive system also needs attention. It is recommended to maintain a regular diet and eat more white foods (white radish, white fungus, pear).

Health preservation focus

  • Pay attention to the body parts corresponding to the metal element in the Five Elements
  • In 2026, the fire element is strong; pay attention to cardiovascular health and blood pressure fluctuations.
  • Maintain a regular schedule and avoid excessive fatigue

Suggestions for Improving Luck and Resolving Problems

Key points for Rooster zodiac in 2026: First, focus on the water element; wearing black or dark blue clothing and accessories helps to reduce fire and protect metal. Second, minimize high-profile behavior; being humble and low-key is the best protection. Third, interact more with friends who are Dragon (Chen) or Snake (Si) signs (Si, You, Chou form a gold combination to enhance metal energy). Fourth, it is recommended to pay respects to Tai Sui at the beginning of the year and pray for safety and smoothness.

Specific operation

  • Carry corresponding Five Elements lucky charms with you
  • Avoid making major decisions in inauspicious directions.
  • Do more good deeds and accumulate virtues to increase positive energy.
