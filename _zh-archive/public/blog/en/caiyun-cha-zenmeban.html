Why I Always Lack Money: Bazi Astrology Helps You Find the Root Cause of Your Financial Luck

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

The salary has increased, but the money is still not enough. Or just when you're about to save up a sum of money, an unexpected expense suddenly appears. Or business always encounters a setback just when it is about to become profitable.

This feeling is hard to explain with the level of effort. In fate analysis, there is a systematic logic for analyzing wealth luck, which helps you find where the real root lies.

What determines wealth luck in the Eight Characters (Bazi)?

In astrology, the Wealth Star represents your relationship with wealth. Whether the Wealth Star is prosperous, whether it has storage to collect, and whether it is being attacked by other forces determine a person's basic financial pattern.

Characteristics of a destiny with good financial luck

  • A prosperous wealth star with roots represents stable financial resources.
  • Wealth entering the treasury (Wealth Star entering Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei) represents the ability to accumulate and retain wealth.
  • The Day Master is strong, capable of shouldering wealth, and abundance of wealth does not scatter.
  • The combination of the Seal Star and the Wealth Star is reasonable, with wealth and ability supporting each other.

Common destiny reasons for poor financial luck

  • Strong peers overcome wealth: In the natal chart, the 'Peer/Companion' elements (same type) are excessively strong, and wealth is dispersed. There is a tendency to lose money, be deceived, or have wealth taken by others.
  • The wealth star is in voidMoney comes and goes, it can't be kept; even if earned, it is easy to spend or face unexpected expenses.
  • The Day Master is too weak to bear wealth.: In the 'wealthy but physically weak' pattern, having money instead brings pressure and trouble, and wealth cannot be carried away
  • The wealth star has been combined awayThe Wealth Star is combined away by other Earthly Branches, cutting off the source of wealth, resulting in unstable income.

The position and condition of the Wealth Star in the natal chart determine the 'pattern' of your financial luck; the Luck Pillars and annual fortune determine the 'timing' of your financial luck. Only by combining the two can you see the full picture of your financial fortune.

Always unable to save money, what is the reason according to fate?

"Can earn but cannot save" usually has the following reasons in fate theory:

  • Wealth star has no storageIn the fate chart, the absence of Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei (wealth storage) means that wealth comes and goes, with no place to accumulate.
  • Repeated rivalryAs soon as money is received, it is 'taken away,' which may manifest as large expenses for social engagements, borrowed money not being repaid, or needing to support family members.
  • Excessive food and injury overpowers the sealActive mind, spends money lavishly, enjoys the present, lacks a sense of saving

People with poor financial luck, when will they encounter a turning point?

A year of prosperous wealth

If the heavenly stems or earthly branches of the year contain the wealth star from your destiny chart, it is a signal that your financial luck will improve that year. Opportunities to increase income will increase, and you can take the initiative to act.

Year of Opening the Treasury

When the earthly branch of the year clashes with the wealth storage in the life chart (for example, if the chart has Chen, and the year's branch meets Xu clashing with Chen), it is a high-probability time node for a surge in wealth luck.

India Transport Grand Luck

When fortunate luck is in play, both nobleman luck and ability luck are improving, making it the best time to build a foundation for wealth and increase income.

Improve financial luck, what suggestions are there in astrology?

  • Understand the attributes of the wealth star in your destiny chart (metal, water, wood, fire, earth), and appropriately enhance the energy of the corresponding colors and directions.
  • People with strong competitive energy should be cautious when starting a business with partners and beware of situations where profits are divided.
  • People whose Wealth Star lacks a treasury should consciously develop saving and financial management habits to make up for the deficiencies in their fate chart.
  • Find your prosperous financial year through charting, and focus on developing new income sources in that year.
