How to make up for a lack of Earth in the Five Elements? A complete guide to stabilizing your foundation and improving fortune

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-24 · Bazi Guide

In the Five Elements, Earth occupies the center and represents stability, tolerance, trust, and carrying capacity. It is the most important harmonizing force among the Five Elements. Those who lack Earth often have unstable foundations, lack a sense of security and credibility, but can effectively improve through systematic methods.

The meaning of earth in the Five Elements

  • Stability and foundation: When the Earth element is stable, those lacking Earth tend to have unstable lives and a lack of sense of security
  • Credit and honesty: Earth master faith, those lacking earth sometimes act inconsistently, making it difficult to gain long-term trust from others
  • Inclusion and ToleranceThe Earth element leader is tolerant, while those lacking the Earth element are sometimes narrow-minded and find it difficult to accept different opinions.
  • Body correspondence: Spleen, stomach, muscles, lips

Judgment of Lack of Earth in the Five Elements

Characters representing Earth in the Bazi: Heavenly Stems Wu and Ji, Earthly Branches Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei. Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei are the four Earth reservoirs, each containing hidden stems, which are the main sources of Earth's strength. If Wu, Ji, Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei are all absent in the Four Pillars, it is basically confirmed that Earth is lacking.

Personality traits lacking earth

  • Lacking a sense of security, full of anxiety about the future
  • Weak resilience, easily collapses when facing difficulties
  • Sometimes words are not trustworthy, and promises are easily compromised.
  • Spleen and stomach function is relatively weak, and the digestive system is prone to problems

Putty Method: Color and Orientation

  • Favorable colorsYellow, brown, earthy color, beige, orange-yellow
  • Favorable direction: Center (Central Earth), Northeast (Gen Earth), Southwest (Kun Earth)
  • Application RecommendationsYellow tablecloths, brown wooden furniture, earth-toned wall paint

Method of applying putty: contact with soil and the earth

The most direct way to repair the soil is to come into contact with real land:

  • Gardening: Planting vegetables or flowers by hand, touching the soil directly
  • Walking barefoot on grass or soil (grounding)
  • Choose a living environment close to the mountains
  • Pottery and ceramics making are excellent filler activities.
  • Seasonality: Every year in the lunar months of March (Wuchen month), June (Jiwei month), September (Wuxu month), and December (Jichou month) is the peak season for Earth, suitable for making important decisions.

Method of nourishing the body: dietary adjustment

  • yellow foodPumpkin, corn, soybeans, potatoes, turmeric
  • Sweet foods(In appropriate amounts): Sweet potatoes, Chinese yam, jujubes
  • Ingredients for Strengthening the Spleen and Nourishing the Stomach: Yam porridge, coix seed, poria, gorgon fruit
  • Having a regular diet and eating three meals at fixed times is the simplest way to tonify the earth and nourish the spleen and stomach.

Putty Methods: Career Choices

  • Real estate, land development (landowner property)
  • Agriculture, Food, Catering
  • Financial Credit (Landlord Credit)
  • Intermediary, mediation, and coordination work (landowner mediates)
  • Traditional culture and historical research (deep-rooted and accumulated)

Putty Method: Talisman

  • Yellow crystal, tiger's eye, yellow tourmaline
  • Ceramic ornaments (pure natural materials)
  • Auspicious animals for the Earth zodiac: Ox (Chou Earth), Dragon (Chen Earth), Goat (Wei Earth), Dog (Xu Earth)

The fundamental method of nourishing the Earth element: establish stable routines in daily life (work and rest, diet, exercise), keep your word, and enhance your personal integrity. This is the deepest way to nourish the Earth element from the inside out.
