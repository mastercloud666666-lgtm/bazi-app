Eight Characters Marriage Matching: A Complete Guide to Emotional Compatibility

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-04 · Bazi Guide

Bazi matchmaking is the practice of comparing and analyzing the birth dates and times of two people to evaluate the depth of their affinity, interaction patterns, and marriage quality from the perspective of fate. This method has a history of over a thousand years in China and is still an important tool that many people refer to before marriage.

The Core Principle of Marriage Compatibility

Marriage compatibility is not simply about comparing the merits of two birth charts, but about analyzing the interactive relationship between the two people's destinies: whether they generate and support each other, whether conflicts arise, and whether each person's useful elements are compatible.

A good marital combination is usually reflected in: the two people's Five Elements complementing each other rather than clashing, their Day Pillars having harmony and affection, and their fortune trends moving in the same direction (one will not be extremely favorable while the other is extremely unfavorable).

Day Pillar Marriage Compatibility: The Most Basic Method

The Day Pillar is the core pillar in the Eight Characters that represents the marriage palace. The Day Stem represents oneself, and the Day Branch represents the spouse palace. When matching marriages, the focus is on:

  • Whether the day branches of both sides form a Six Harmony (Zi-Chou pair, Yin-Hai pair, Mao-Xu pair, Chen-You pair, Si-Shen pair, Wu-Wei pair)
  • Do the day branches of both sides clash in the six conflicts (Zi-Wu clash, Chou-Wei clash, Yin-Shen clash, Mao-You clash, Chen-Xu clash, Si-Hai clash) — marriages with six conflicts are often unstable.
  • Whether the Five Elements relationship of both parties' day stems is mutually generating

💡 The six clashes of the day branch do not absolutely mean a bad marriage. Some combinations 'clash to move, move to change,' which can actually bring passion and growth. The key is the overall coordination of the life chart.

Spouse Star Analysis

In the Eight Characters (Ba Zi), for a man, the 'Wealth Star' represents his wife, while for a woman, the 'Officer Star' represents her husband. By analyzing the strength, position, and condition of the spouse star in the natal chart, one can determine:

  • General characteristics of a spouse (appearance, temperament, personality type)
  • Timing of Marriage (Which Luck Cycle and Year Are Most Likely for Getting Married)
  • Marriage quality (stable type, turbulent type, or multi-phase type)

Reference Dimensions for the Fortune of Marriage Matching

Professional marriage compatibility analysis usually makes comprehensive judgments from the following dimensions:

  1. Degree of mutual complementarity among the Five Elements
  2. The situation of Six Harmonies and Six Clashes of the Day Pillar
  3. Whether the deities used by both parties are mutually favorable deities
  4. Whether the luck trend is synchronized
  5. Stability of the Marriage Palace (Day Branch)

How should the results of a marriage compatibility test be interpreted?

The conclusion of a marriage compatibility assessment is not a simple 'good' or 'bad', but rather provides a probabilistic reference. Even if the results show conflicts, through mutual understanding, communication, and effort, a happy marriage can still be achieved; conversely, even the most ideal combination, if not cherished by both parties, can still lead to separation.
