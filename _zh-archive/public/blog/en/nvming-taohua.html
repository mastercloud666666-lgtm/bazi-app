Women's Peach Blossom Star: Detailed Explanation of the Bazi Love Code

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-16 · Bazi Guide

The Peach Blossom Star is a divine star in the Eight Characters of Destiny that is most directly related to emotions and relationships with the opposite sex. For a woman's fate, the position, strength, and condition of the Peach Blossom Star often reveal the richness of her emotional life and the characteristics of her relationship patterns.

The calculation method of Peach Blossom Star

Peach Blossom Star (also known as 'Xianchi') is calculated based on the year branch or the day branch:

  • If the earthly branch of the year or day is Shen, Zi, or Chen: the peach blossom is in You
  • If the earthly branch of the year or day is Yin, Wu, or Xu: the peach blossom is in Mao.
  • If the annual branch or the daily branch is Hai, Mao, or Wei: the peach blossom is in Zi.
  • If the earthly branch of the year or day is Si, You, or Chou: the peach blossom is in Wu

If the above corresponding earthly branches appear in the destiny chart, it means the fate carries 'peach blossoms'.

The Position and Meaning of Peach Blossoms

Annual Branch Peach Blossom (External Peach Blossom)

Emotional relationships are extensive, with good affinity with the opposite sex, and socially active. Early romantic experiences are rich, but early love can easily interfere with studies.

Monthly Branch Peach Blossom

Emotional energy is abundant, making it easy to encounter someone you like in work and social circles.

Day Branch Peach Blossom (Peach Blossom Within the Wall)

The most stable type of peach blossom, with peach blossom energy manifested in marital relationships – a strong attraction to one's partner and a relatively good quality of emotional life in marriage.

Hour Branch Peach Blossom

Emotions remain active in later years, which may also indicate that the relationship with children carries traits of romance.

💡 The Peach Blossom on the Day Branch (domestic peach blossom) and the Peach Blossom on the Year Branch (external peach blossom) have different characteristics. External peach blossoms bring a colorful love life but can also lead to complicated relationships; domestic peach blossoms often help with a proper relationship, leading to sweet feelings within marriage.

Characteristics of women with a strong Peach Blossom luck

Women with a strong and flourishing romantic fate usually have the following traits:

  • Attractive appearance, outstanding temperament
  • Has strong social skills and is well-liked
  • Rich emotional life, many friends of the opposite sex
  • If peach blossoms and official stars appear together, both romantic opportunities and ideal relationships are excellent.

The negative effects of peach blossoms

When peach blossoms are overly abundant or situated in an unfavorable configuration, they may also bring emotional troubles:

  • Peach blossoms appear together with financial misfortune: relationships are easily interfered with by a third party
  • Peach Blossom Sitting in Emptiness: Emotional relationships are illusory and unreal, difficult to take root.
  • Peach blossoms are broken through: relationships are prone to end without progress

The timing for activating romantic luck

The following situations can activate peach blossom energy and increase romantic opportunities:

  • When the luck cycle or annual fortune shows a Peach Blossom Earthly Branch in the natal chart
  • In the given year, the flow of time and the fate chart's romantic stars form a trine or sextile.
  • Experiencing a favorable official luck cycle while also encountering a year of romantic opportunities
