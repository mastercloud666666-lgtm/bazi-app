Constantly unwell: Bazi astrology helps you find the health weaknesses in your birth chart

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

Always sleeping poorly, or with the same lifestyle, others are full of energy while you are always tired. Repeatedly getting the same type of illness, or a certain part of your body always having problems, cured only for it to return.

At the intersection of traditional Chinese medicine and fate analysis, there is a method that analyzes constitutional weaknesses through the Five Elements, helping you find the root of the problem.

The Correspondence Between the Five Elements and Body Organs

In the study of fate and destiny, the Five Elements have a direct correspondence with human organs (consistent with the traditional Chinese medicine theory of the five viscera):

  • WoodLiver and gallbladder, muscles and bones, eyes, nervous system
  • fireHeart, small intestine, blood circulation, vision
  • soil: Spleen and stomach, digestive system, muscles, skin
  • Gold: lungs, large intestine, respiratory system, skin
  • WaterKidneys, bladder, reproductive system, skeleton, ears

If a certain element in the natal chart is too weak or too strong, the health risks of the corresponding organs will increase accordingly.

Common Weak Points in the Natal Chart Health

Weak water — kidney deficiency, poor sleep, strong sense of fear

If the natal chart lacks water or water is restrained, leading to insufficient kidney energy, one is prone to: insomnia with vivid dreams, soreness in the lower back and knees, recurring urinary system issues, and strong feelings of anxiety and fear.

Wood Weakness — Liver Qi Stagnation, Emotional Suppression

If the natal chart lacks wood or wood is restrained by metal, the liver energy is not smooth, manifesting as: emotional suppression, prone to depression, shallow sleep, dry eyes, and irregular menstruation (in women).

Weak Earth — Poor spleen and stomach, digestive problems

If the birth chart lacks earth or if earth is overcome by wood, the spleen and stomach functions are weak, easily leading to: indigestion, frequent diarrhea or constipation, poor appetite, and blood sugar problems.

Weak fire—heart under stress, low mood

In the birth chart, the fire element is weak, leading to insufficient heart blood supply, manifested as: palpitations, low blood pressure, persistent low mood, and easily cold hands and feet.

Metal Weakness — Lungs and Respiratory System Sensitive

If the natal chart lacks metal or if metal is overcome by fire, the lung metal is weak, making one prone to: recurrent respiratory infections, skin allergies, and large intestine dysfunction.

The strength and weakness of the Five Elements in a person's fate chart determine their innate physical tendencies. It does not necessarily mean they will get sick, but understanding the weak points allows for more targeted prevention.

How do the major and minor luck cycles affect health

Even if the natal chart indicates a relatively healthy pattern, during certain major luck periods and annual cycles, the corresponding organs may experience increased pressure:

  • In a year when the cycle clashes with a weak element in the fate chart, the corresponding organs experience greater stress.
  • Before and after the major luck cycle changes, the body's resistance decreases, making it easy for health issues to appear during this period.
  • When the annual fortune forms a three-punishment relationship with a certain element in the natal chart, chronic problems of the corresponding organ are likely to flare up in that year.

Adjust your lifestyle according to your destiny chart

In terms of destiny-related health advice, the core is 'nourish the weak and reduce the strong'.

  • Lacking water in the fate chart (kidney deficiency constitution): get more rest, ensure adequate sleep, reduce excessive consumption, and pay attention to the intake of black foods (black sesame, black beans) in the diet.
  • Weak wood in the natal chart (liver-depressed constitution): Engage in more outdoor activities, increase green vegetables, and avoid prolonged emotional suppression without release.
  • Birth chart with weak Earth (weak spleen and stomach): eat regularly, reduce raw and cold foods, and avoid excessive worrying
  • Weak fire in the life chart (high heart stress): ensure adequate exercise, keep warm, and avoid long-term staying up late

Of course, the above suggestions are for reference only. If there are substantial physical problems, please seek medical attention promptly. Fate analysis cannot replace medical treatment.
