How to Supplement Water in the Five Elements? Methods to Activate Wisdom and Flexibility

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-24 · Bazi Guide

Water is the most dynamic element among the Five Elements, representing wisdom, flexibility, adaptability, and inner depth. Those who lack water often have deficiencies in these areas, but they can be effectively compensated through the right methods. This article provides you with a systematic Five Elements water supplementation plan.

The meaning of Water in the Five Elements

  • Wisdom and LearningWater governs intelligence; those who lack water have relatively insufficient learning ability and the ability to infer from one case to another.
  • Flexible and adaptableWater is changeable; those who lack water are sometimes too stubborn and find it difficult to adapt to change.
  • Depth and introspection: Those who are governed by water are profound; those lacking water sometimes lack deep thinking.
  • Body correspondence: Kidneys, bladder, bones, ears

Characteristics and Behavioral Traits of Water Deficiency

  • Thinking is relatively linear and lacks flexibility.
  • Has a weak sense of self-protection and can sometimes be too naive
  • The inner world is not rich enough, and the depth of emotions needs to be cultivated.
  • Easily anxious when things happen, lacking the composure to remain calm in the face of change.

Watering Methods: Colors and Directions

  • Favorable colorsBlack, dark blue, dark gray, navy blue
  • Favorable direction: North (North belongs to Water)
  • Application Recommendations: Deep blue or black clothing and accessories, the bedroom can be accented with dark elements

Hydration method: Contact with real water elements

  • Place an aquarium at home (pay attention to the feng shui position, usually in the north or southeast).
  • Often go for walks by the sea, lake, or stream
  • Swimming is the most direct way to replenish water.
  • Choose a living environment close to a water source (riverside, lakeside community)
  • Winter (the season of abundant water) is a good time to adjust direction

Hydration method: dietary adjustment

  • Black food: black sesame, black beans, black rice, wood ear mushrooms, black-boned chicken
  • Salty food(In appropriate amounts): sea kelp, nori, sea fish, and other seafood products
  • Kidney-tonifying ingredients: Chinese yam, goji berries, walnuts (tonify kidney qi)
  • Drink more waterMaintain an adequate daily water intake, the most direct way to hydrate.

Hydration Methods: Career Choices

  • Finance, trade, logistics (the flow of water circulation and wealth)
  • Scientific research, philosophy, psychology (intense deep thinking)
  • Shipping, tourism, and aquaculture-related industries
  • IT, Internet (information flows like water)

Hydration method: mascot

  • Obsidian, Black Tourmaline: Hydration and Protection
  • Aquamarine, Sapphire: Empowered by Water Energy
  • Auspicious animals for the Water zodiac: Rat (Zi Water), Pig (Hai Water)

Special reminder about hydration: Those with abundant water (such as born in the month of Zi or Hai, or with a BaZi chart with a lot of water) actually do not need to supplement water. Be sure to first confirm your favorable element, and do not blindly hydrate.
