Fortune in the Eight Characters: Which Type of Wealth—Regular or Extra—is More Prosperous

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-05 · Bazi Guide

Wealth luck is one of the most frequently asked topics in Bazi astrology. Many people want to know: Do they have wealth luck? When is their wealth luck at its peak? Is it better to have regular wealth or unexpected wealth? This article answers these questions from the perspective of astrology.

Wealth Stars in the Eight Characters: Proper Wealth and Partial Wealth

In the Ten Gods system, wealth stars are divided into two categories:

  • Regular income:The Five Elements that the Day Master controls, and that differ in yin and yang. They represent stable income and legitimate wealth, accumulated through diligent and steady effort.
  • Windfall Income:The Five Elements that the Day Master controls, and that share the same Yin and Yang. They represent unexpected wealth, speculative gains, and also signify the relationship with the father.

For example: If the Day Master is Jia Wood, it overcomes Earth, then Wu Earth (Yang Earth) is considered偏财 (indirect wealth), and Ji Earth (Yin Earth) is considered正财 (direct wealth).

Characteristics of a Wealth-Focused Fate

People with prosperous regular income are usually practical and diligent, with stable sources of wealth—such as salary, rent, and fixed income. They are conservative in financial management, dislike taking risks, and accumulate wealth slowly but steadily. People with a regular income pattern are suitable for running steady businesses or working in large enterprises.

Characteristics of a Wealth-Favoring Destiny

People with strong windfall wealth often have keen insight, are good at seizing opportunities, and have a strong wealth growth potential. They may acquire substantial wealth through investments, sales, brokerage, or side businesses, but money comes and goes, and their ability to retain wealth is relatively weak. People with a windfall wealth pattern are suitable for entrepreneurship, freelancing, or the financial industry.

💡 Regular income represents 'steady and continuous flow', while windfall income represents 'big ups and downs'. Each pattern has its advantages and disadvantages; the key is to plan according to your own financial type, rather than forcing a windfall-type person to follow a regular income path.

Characteristics of a Fate Favorable to Wealth

If the Eight Characters indicate good financial luck, they usually have one of the following characteristics:

  • The wealth star is strong and is supported by the Beneficial God of Food and the Hurting Officer.
  • The treasury (Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei) is clashed open in the natal chart or encounters a combination in the luck cycle or annual fortune.
  • The day master is strong and can control abundant wealth.
  • Both wealth and official position are in harmony, with a clear structure

Characteristics of a destiny with poor financial luck

  • Wealth star is largely deprived of money by Rival stars (Brother stars)
  • If the Day Master is too weak, encountering wealth becomes a harm (too much wealth with a weak self)
  • The wealth star is overly drained by the Output and Hurting Star
  • In the life chart, all wealth stars are either completely buried or obstructed by combinations.

How to view the peak period of financial luck

The peak of financial luck usually occurs at the following points:

  • Experiencing the luck of Food God and Hurting Officer, the power to generate wealth is strong
  • The annual luck encounters a clash with the wealth storage (for example, if there is Chen in the destiny, the annual luck encounters Xu clashing with Chen)
  • The annual wealth star is in harmony with the Life Chart's useful god
