# 冥書儀象 · Arcane Script Apparatus

## Design Philosophy

This is a cosmological instrument, not a form. The interface exists inside an observatory — not a website. Every element is borrowed from the visual language of celestial cartography: the precision of astronomical diagrams, the solemnity of bronze astronomical instruments, the dense accumulation of ancient star charts. The composition must feel as though it was drawn over centuries, layer by layer, by someone with access to knowledge most cannot see.

Space is the primary material. Deep midnight — near-black navy with the faintest blue undertone — is not a background color but a substance, like deep water or night sky. Against this void, each element earns its place through luminance alone. Nothing is placed for decoration. The central mandala is not a graphic — it is the actual apparatus, a living cosmological wheel that the consultation orbits. It dominates. It breathes. It takes the right two-thirds of the canvas and fills it with concentric geometry, radiating lines, and glowing cardinal markers. The result of painstaking geometric construction, as if drawn with compass and straightedge over countless iterations.

Gold is the language of revelation — used with extreme scarcity. A single horizontal rule. The center point of the mandala. The accent on the submit action. Nothing else is gold. Blue is the language of the cosmos — deep blue for authority, light blue for ambient luminance, the faintest blue-white for atmospheric glow. The entire palette contains no more than five values. Every tonal relationship is the product of deep expertise, calibrated so that each element sits at exactly the right luminance level to read without effort.

Typography is sparse to the point of ceremony. The brand name "天機命理" appears once, large, light-weight, wide letter-spacing — not as a logo but as a declaration carved in darkness. Below it, a single thin gold line, then a whispered Latin italic. Everything else — labels, navigation, annotations — exists at minimum readable scale, almost clinical, like instrument markings. The consultation panel on the left is a narrow strip of slightly lighter dark: a side console beside the great wheel, where the practitioner enters coordinates.

The form is not a form — it is a coordinate input panel. Fields are minimal lines, not boxes. Labels are tiny instrument-style markings. The submit action is a single luminous band of deep blue with a gold terminal mark. The overall impression: you are not filling out a web form, you are calibrating an ancient instrument, entering the exact celestial coordinates of your birth moment. This is the product of master-level execution — every spacing decision, every opacity value, every font size exists because it could not be otherwise.
