Common Questions

Clear delivery, clear billing, and clear limits.

What is free?

The general Daily Almanac is free. It includes traditional calendar information for the day and does not require your birth date.

What does the paid membership add?

The Personal Monthly BaZi Forecast compares your natal chart with the annual and solar-month pillars. It costs $9.90 monthly or $69 yearly.

Can I stop either email?

Yes. Every free Daily Almanac includes a one-click stop link. Paid members can pause monthly forecasts without ending paid access, or cancel renewal separately in PayPal.

Is the forecast a prediction?

No. It converts traditional chart relationships into a reflective planning framework. It does not guarantee outcomes or replace medical, financial, legal, or other professional advice.
