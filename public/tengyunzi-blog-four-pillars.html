Four Pillars of Destiny Explained

The Four Pillars are the structural foundation of BaZi: year, month, day, and hour, each written as a Heavenly Stem and an Earthly Branch.

  • Beginner
  • Chart structure
  • 9 min read

A BaZi chart contains four vertical columns. Each column has a top character and a bottom character. Together they form eight characters, which is why BaZi literally means eight characters.

What Each Pillar Represents

PillarCommon life areaHow to read it
YearBackground, ancestry, public layerContext and early environment
MonthCareer, parents, seasonThe strongest environmental force
DaySelf and intimate lifeThe Day Master sits here
HourLater life, children, outputPrivate aspiration and long-range direction

Stem and Branch

The Heavenly Stem shows visible elemental expression. The Earthly Branch is deeper and more seasonal. Branches may contain hidden stems, which is one reason BaZi can be more layered than a simple zodiac reading.

Why the Day Master Matters

The Day Master is the Heavenly Stem of the Day Pillar. Every other symbol is interpreted in relationship to it, so two people with similar pillars can still read very differently.

The chart is not a pile of symbols. It is a relationship map centered on the Day Master.
