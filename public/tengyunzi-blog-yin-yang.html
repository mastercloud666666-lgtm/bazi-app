Yin and Yang in BaZi

Yin and yang describe polarity, not moral value. In BaZi, every element has a yang expression and a yin expression.

  • Foundation
  • Polarity
  • 7 min read

Yang tends to be more direct, outward, initiating, and visible. Yin tends to be more subtle, receptive, adaptive, and interior. Both are necessary.

Polarity, Not Ranking

Yang is not better than yin. Yin is not weaker than yang. They describe different ways energy behaves.

Five Elements, Ten Expressions

Each of the Five Elements splits into yin and yang. This creates the ten Heavenly Stems and ten Day Masters.

ElementYangYin
Wood甲 tall tree乙 vine or flower
Fire丙 sun丁 candle
Earth戊 mountain己 garden soil
Metal庚 axe辛 jewelry
Water壬 ocean癸 mist

How It Changes a Reading

Two people can both be Wood Day Masters but express very differently. Yang Wood stands like a tree. Yin Wood adapts like a vine. The element is shared; the polarity changes the style.

Yin and yang are the rhythm of the chart: push and receive, reveal and conceal, begin and refine.
