Forecast questions

Is this the same as the monthly email membership?

No. This is a one-time, Tengyunzi-written PDF covering all twelve months. The membership is an automated recurring forecast sent as each new solar month begins.

When does the twelve-month period begin?

The report is prepared for the next useful twelve-solar-month window from the order date unless your question names a specific calendar year.

Does it guarantee that an event will happen?

No. It identifies stronger support, higher friction, and better planning postures. Your resources, obligations, market conditions, and choices still matter.

What if my birth hour is unknown?

The forecast can still use the year, month, and day pillars. Hour-dependent conclusions are clearly limited.

Tengyunzi uses BaZi as a planning framework, not a guarantee of future events.
