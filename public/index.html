How BaZi Works

A chart is a map of patterns, not a sentence about fate

BaZi reads your birth year, month, day, and hour as a symbolic structure. The useful question is not "what will happen to me", but "what pattern keeps repeating, and how do I work with it better".

  • Four PillarsYear, month, day, and hour form the base chart.
  • Day MasterThe center of the reading, used to interpret the rest of the chart.
  • Five ElementsWood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water describe balance, stress, and expression.
  • Luck CyclesTiming cycles help you read seasons of expansion, pressure, and change.
