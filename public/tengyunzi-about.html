About Tengyunzi

Standard BaZi and Eastern philosophy, explained in clear modern English for practical decisions.

Structure first. Meaning second. Timing third. Action last.

How I got here

From symbols to a language people can actually use

Tengyunzi began with a simple frustration: many BaZi explanations either sounded too mystical or too mechanical. People could get a chart, but not a usable way to think with it. This site was built to bridge that gap - traditional Chinese metaphysics, translated into clear English for people making modern decisions.

The work sits between study and coaching. It respects the old system, but it does not hide behind old words. A chart should help you notice your own operating pattern: where you move easily, where pressure repeats, where timing changes the quality of a choice, and where attention needs protection.

What BaZi actually is

A map of timing, temperament, and recurring pressure

BaZi, also called Four Pillars of Destiny, maps the year, month, day, and hour of birth into Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches. From there, the system reads the Day Master, Five Elements, Ten Gods, Luck Pillars, and annual or monthly cycles.

I treat BaZi as a pattern language. It is not a replacement for judgment, therapy, medicine, law, or financial advice. It is a structured way to ask better questions about work, relationships, money, creativity, discipline, recovery, and timing.
