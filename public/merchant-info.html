About the service

Tengyunzi interprets calculated birth-chart data in standard BaZi order, including Day Master strength, Five Elements, hidden stems, Ten Gods, natal interactions, Shen Sha, Luck Pillars, and annual timing. Interpretations are traditional cultural readings rather than guaranteed events or medical, legal, financial, investment, or psychological advice.
