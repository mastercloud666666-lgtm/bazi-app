服务说明 · About the Service

Yunzi Culture provides digital personality-analysis content based on traditional Chinese Ganzhi (Five-Element) culture. Reports are generated from the birth information the user submits, and are intended to help users understand their personality traits, talents, strengths and suitable directions from a cultural perspective. This is a self-understanding tool for cultural reference — not fortune-telling, divination or prediction — and does not constitute medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.
