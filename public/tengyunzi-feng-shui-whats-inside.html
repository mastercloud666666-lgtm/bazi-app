Rule-based AI report

Ten connected parts. One consistent spatial verdict.

The image model identifies only what can be seen in the plan. Sitting, facing, Five-Element relations, palace effects, room conclusions, and adjustment directions are produced by the fixed Feng Shui rule engine.

01Plan Facts

Floor-Plan and Direction Check

North edge, whole-house facing, floor number, room boundaries, beds, major windows, doors, facilities, and occupant assignments are checked before interpretation begins.

02Whole Home

Whole-House Sitting and Facing

The largest balcony or window wall establishes the facing direction. The opposite side becomes sitting, creating the reference for the entire property.

03Five Elements

Whole-House Five-Element Pattern

The sitting element and facing element are compared directly to name the property's Wealth, Officer, Resource, Output, or Companion pattern.

04Palace Map

Tai Ji Center and Eight-Sector Overlay

The floor is read from its own center. Each direction is assigned its trigram, Five Element, household role, and the rooms or facilities occupying that sector.

05Building Form

Structural Palace Findings

Active conditions such as a Northwest kitchen, an East toilet, and missing palace sectors are stated directly, together with the role affected on the analysed floor.

06Household Roles

Occupants and Floor-Specific Effects

Married adults, sons, and daughters are assigned by family role. An only son is treated as the eldest son, and palace effects apply only to occupants living on that floor.

07Room Analysis

Room Sitting and Facing

Bedrooms use the bedhead as sitting. Rooms without beds use the largest window for facing; when no window exists, the door sets the facing direction.

08Energy Balance

Doors, Windows, and Energy Points

Secondary openings are evaluated against the room's sitting and facing. When one side is drained or over-strengthened, the report names the direction in which an energy point should be added.

09Residence Hexagram

Person-to-Palace Hexagram

The occupant's trigram is placed above the room-palace trigram. Verified traditional verdicts are shown; unresolved combinations are marked for personal review.

10Action Plan

Priority Adjustments and Verification

Structural issues are ranked before room energy adjustments. Unclear geometry, missing corners, and matters that cannot be corrected by an energy point are sent to manual service.

Interior scope

The AI report assesses the uploaded residential interior floor plan. It does not assess roads, neighbouring buildings, landform, water, or the wider development. For external Feng Shui, request a personal consultation.
