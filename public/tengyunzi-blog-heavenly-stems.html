The Ten Heavenly Stems

Heavenly Stems are the visible elemental layer of a BaZi chart. The Day Stem is especially important because it becomes the Day Master.

  • Symbols
  • Stems
  • 9 min read

Each stem combines an element with yin or yang. This creates ten expressions: two Wood, two Fire, two Earth, two Metal, and two Water.

Day Stem as Day Master

The stem on the Day Pillar represents you in the chart. Every other stem, branch, and Ten God is interpreted relative to this point.

Visible Energy

Stems show what is more visible or easier to name. Branches often describe deeper seasonal roots and hidden motivations.

Stems are the chart's visible language. Branches are where that language grows roots.
