Required information

A useful review begins with a complete plan.

Please prepare a readable floor plan. Hand-drawn plans are accepted when the proportions, dimensions, doors, windows, room names, and compass direction are clearly marked.

01 · Floor plan

The complete property layout

Include every room, hallway, balcony, entrance, bathroom, kitchen, storage area, and any missing or irregular section.

02 · Orientation

Accurate facing direction

Mark north and provide the measured facing direction or compass degrees. State where and how the measurement was taken.

03 · Openings

Doors and windows

Show the main entrance, internal doors, balcony doors, windows, large openings, and the direction each door opens when relevant.

04 · Dimensions

Room and total area

Provide the total property area and room dimensions in square metres or square feet. Approximate measurements should be labelled as approximate.

05 · People

Occupants and room use

Explain who lives or works there, which rooms they use, and any important changes such as moving, renovation, a new role, or a new relationship.

06 · Objective

What you want to adjust

Choose the priorities that matter now and describe the current problem in concrete, observable terms.
