How the AI report works

Direction first. Fixed rules for every conclusion.

The image model extracts visible geometry only. Whole-home sitting and facing, room micro-patterns, household palaces, Five-Element relations, and adjustment types are calculated by a fixed rule engine.
Whole home

Main opening sets the facing

The opposite direction becomes the sitting side. Their Five-Element relation determines the whole-house pattern.

Bedrooms

Bedhead sets the sitting

The wall behind the pillows is sitting. The bed-foot direction is facing. Doors and secondary windows become energy points.

Rooms without beds

Largest window comes first

The largest window sets facing. If there is no window, the door sets facing. Smaller openings remain energy points.

Household palaces

People are assigned by family role

An only son is treated as the eldest son. Floor-specific effects apply only to people living on the analysed floor.
