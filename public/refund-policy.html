退款与取消政策 · Refund & Cancellation Policy

最后更新 Last updated: 2026-07-04. 我们希望你在付款前清楚了解服务性质、交付方式与退款边界。 We want you to understand the service, delivery and refund terms before paying.

1. 可申请退款 · Eligible for Refund

  • 同一订单重复扣款。 Duplicate charge for the same order.
  • 付款成功但系统未生成报告，且无法补交付。 Payment succeeded but the report was not generated and cannot be re-delivered.
  • 因网站技术故障无法履行服务，且无法通过订单找回或补发解决。 A technical failure prevented delivery and cannot be resolved by recovery or re-issue.
  • 实际扣款金额与页面展示不一致且经核验为系统错误。 The amount charged differs from the displayed price due to a confirmed system error.

2. 通常不予退款 · Generally Non-Refundable

  • 数字报告已成功生成并可查看（虚拟数字内容一经交付即视为已使用）。 A digital report has been generated and is viewable (delivered digital content is deemed consumed).
  • 因个人理解、主观感受或对传统文化的不同看法而要求无理由退款。 Refund requested purely due to subjective feelings or differing views on traditional culture.
  • 因用户填写错误的出生信息导致内容不符预期（可联系客服协助重新处理）。 Content mismatch caused by incorrect birth info entered by the user (contact support to re-process).

3. 会员订阅：取消与退款 · Membership: Cancellation & Refunds

Cancel anytime. You can cancel an auto-renewing PayPal membership at any time in your PayPal account (Settings → Payments → Automatic payments), or by emailing us. Cancellation stops all future renewals; your benefits remain active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds. Membership grants immediate unlimited digital access, so fees for the current period are generally non-refundable once the period has started. As a courtesy, if an auto-renewal charged you and you have not used any membership benefit in the new period, contact us within 7 days of that charge and we will review a refund of that renewal. WeChat/Alipay time-passes are one-time and non-refundable once activated.

随时取消。你可随时在 PayPal 账户中取消自动续订会员（设置 → 付款 → 自动付款），或来信取消。取消后不再产生续费，已付周期内权益仍有效至到期。

退款。会员开通后即获得即时的不限次数字权益，当前周期费用在周期开始后通常不予退款。作为善意处理：若自动续订已扣款且你在新周期内未使用任何会员权益，可在扣款后 7 天内联系我们，我们会审核该笔续费的退款。微信/支付宝时长卡为一次性，激活后不予退款。

4. 退款申请方式 · How to Request

Email tgspc2008@163.com or use our Contact page with your order number, payment time, item, and reason. We usually review within 1–3 business days. Approved refunds are returned via the original payment channel; PayPal orders follow PayPal's refund and dispute process.

发邮件至 tgspc2008@163.com 或通过 联系我们 提交，附订单号、付款时间、购买项目与原因。我们通常在 1–3 个工作日内核验。符合条件的退款按原支付渠道退回；PayPal 订单按 PayPal 退款与争议流程执行。
