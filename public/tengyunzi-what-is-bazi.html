The Ancient System

What is BaZi?

BaZi, also known as the Four Pillars of Destiny, is a traditional Chinese system for reading temperament, life timing, and repeating patterns from a person's birth data.

BaZi means eight characters. A chart is built from four columns: the year, month, day, and hour of birth. Each column contains one Heavenly Stem and one Earthly Branch, creating eight symbols in total.

The useful point of BaZi is not to frighten people with fixed predictions. At Tengyunzi, we treat it as a self-knowledge framework: a way to observe personality, pressure, relationships, work style, and timing with more precision.

A complete reading starts with the chart structure, then studies the Five Elements, the Day Master, the Ten Gods, and the Luck Pillars. Together they show both your baseline pattern and the seasons when that pattern changes.

The Four Pillars

The chart is arranged as four pillars. The Day Pillar is the center of the reading because its Heavenly Stem represents the Day Master.

Year 庚 午 金 / 马
Month 壬 午 水 / 马
Day 戊 子 土 / 鼠
Hour 甲 寅 木 / 虎
木 火 土 金 水

Year often describes background and public-facing influences. Month describes season, environment, and career pressure. Day describes the self and intimate life. Hour points toward later life, output, children, and private aspiration.

The Five Elements

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water are the basic language of the chart. Their balance explains how energy moves, where stress collects, and what type of support is useful.

How the Cycles Work

The elements do not sit still. They support one another through a generating cycle and regulate one another through a controlling cycle.

Generating Cycle

This is the flow of support. One element produces the next, like a chain of nourishment.

  • 木 → 火Wood feeds Fire
  • 火 → 土Fire creates Earth
  • 土 → 金Earth bears Metal
  • 金 → 水Metal carries Water
  • 水 → 木Water nourishes Wood

Controlling Cycle

This is the flow of restraint. Control is not bad; it keeps an element from becoming excessive.

  • 木 → 土Wood parts Earth
  • 土 → 水Earth contains Water
  • 水 → 火Water cools Fire
  • 火 → 金Fire refines Metal
  • 金 → 木Metal shapes Wood

The Ten Gods

The Ten Gods translate element relationships into practical life themes. They explain how a chart expresses ambition, pressure, money, creativity, support, and competition.

Companion

Peers, identity, friendship, comparison, and the need to stand beside others without losing yourself.

Output

Expression, talent, speaking, making, teaching, and the way your inner pattern becomes visible.

Wealth

Resources, value, desire, management, action, and the capacity to work with the material world.

Officer

Rules, duty, authority, reputation, pressure, and the structure that asks you to become more precise.

Resource

Learning, protection, recovery, intuition, guidance, and the support that returns you to center.

Luck Pillars

A BaZi chart is not only a fixed birth map. It also unfolds through ten-year chapters that change the elemental weather around your chart.

Luck Pillars are the timing layer of BaZi. Each decade brings a new stem and branch, and that combination interacts with the original chart.

This is why two people with similar personalities can experience very different seasons. A chart may be quiet in one decade, pressured in another, and highly visible in the next.

0-9Early environment and family imprint.
10-19Learning patterns, friendships, and self-image.
20-29Career direction, independence, and first major choices.
30-39Responsibility, relationship patterns, and visible results.
40+Refinement, repositioning, and longer-range meaning.

Questions about BaZi

These are the questions most people ask before they trust a reading enough to enter their birth data.

Is BaZi the same as Chinese zodiac?

No. The zodiac only uses the year animal. BaZi uses year, month, day, hour, stems, branches, hidden stems, elements, Ten Gods, and Luck Pillars.

Do I need my birth hour?

A birth hour makes the chart more complete. If it is unknown, you can still read the year, month, and day structure, but later-life and private themes will be less precise.

Is BaZi a fixed prediction?

We do not use it that way. The chart shows patterns and timing pressures. Your choices, environment, skill, and awareness still matter.

Why does the Day Master matter?

The Day Master is the reference point. Every other symbol is interpreted by its relationship to that central stem, so it anchors the entire reading.
