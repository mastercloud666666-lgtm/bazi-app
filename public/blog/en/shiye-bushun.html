What to do if your career has been going poorly: Fate tells you what comes after the low point

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

You may have changed several jobs and worked hard for many years, but you always feel stuck by some invisible force—you can't get promoted, you can't earn money, and you can't find direction.

This feeling is sometimes not a matter of ability, but a matter of timing. The value of destiny studies lies in helping you discern: whether now is the time to keep pushing or the time to gather strength and wait.

What is the 'career luck low point' in destiny analysis?

In the Eight Characters (Ba Zi), the Officer and Killing stars represent authority, status, and career direction; the Wealth star represents resources and income; the Seal star represents the accumulation of abilities and support from benefactors. When these three are suppressed during major luck cycles and annual fortune, a career often falls into a low point.

Common Fate Signals of a Career Low Point

  • Experiencing a major luck period related to siblings and peersIf the peer and rival stars are strong, they will control wealth, resources will be dispersed, there will easily be competition and internal friction, and income will stagnate.
  • The official star is being challenged and restrained: In the current year, official duties may be affected, making it easy for one's position to change, be demoted, or leave the job
  • Influential star entering the tomb: Abilities cannot be fully utilized, support from influential people weakens, and efforts are not easily recognized
  • The destiny chart has an excessive overabundance of the Output/Expression element: Active thinking but difficult to focus, prone to changing direction frequently, easily distracted

A career setback does not mean it will always be unsuccessful. Major fortunes change every ten years, and annual fortunes change every year. Low points have cycles, and turning points also have cycles.

Why does it still not work despite making an effort?

In fate theory, there is a very important concept called 'time.' The same action can have completely different effects at different stages of fortune:

  • Actively exploring opportunities in years of prosperous financial luck often yields twice the result with half the effort.
  • In years when the official star is being challenged, forcibly vying for a position is more likely to lead to defeat.
  • In years when business is booming, studying to improve yourself and building your network is more effective than directly pushing for performance.

Many people rush forward desperately during low periods, but the more they rush, the more they encounter obstacles. Destiny tells us: understanding the situation and acting accordingly is more important than brute force.

When does a career breakthrough usually happen?

Official Star Annual Luck

A year when your official star appears in the heavenly stems or earthly branches of the flowing year is a high-probability year for promotion, recognition, or new opportunities.

The annual fortune aligns with the major luck of the Seal Luck

During the Great Luck period of the Printing Luck (accumulation phase), when the annual luck encounters the Wealth Star, it is often the time for the 'thick accumulation and thin release' to truly exert its force.

Before and after the major luck cycle change

In the three years before a major fortune shift, the old pattern loosens and the new pattern has not yet stabilized; it is the best window period for taking initiative and changing tracks.

Different life charts are suitable for different career directions.

Besides timing, fate analysis can also help you determine the suitable industry and role:

  • Official seal generates: Suitable for positions within the system, in management, or with authoritative endorsement
  • God of Food Brings Wealth: Suitable for technical creation, freelancing, and entrepreneurship, monetizing based on ability
  • Strong Competitors: Highly competitive, suitable for sales, investment, and confrontational industries; need to pay attention to partnership risks
  • Flourishing Hurting OfficerStrong innovation ability, suitable for design, consulting, performance, and media fields

What can be done now?

If you are in a low point in your career, the suggested approach according to fate theory is:

  • First identify the current major fortune period and annual fortune, and confirm whether it is a low period or about to improve.
  • During low periods, focus on 'accumulating' rather than 'sprinting': learning, building connections, and honing skills
  • Analyze your Bazi to identify the industry direction that truly suits you, and avoid wasting effort on the wrong path.
  • Find the 'benefactor palace' in your destiny chart, understand what type of people are your support, and proactively establish connections with them.
