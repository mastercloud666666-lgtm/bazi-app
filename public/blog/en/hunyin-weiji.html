Marriage Crisis: Bazi Astrology Tells You How Deep This Relationship Still Is

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

The hardest moments in a marriage are not the arguments, but the silence — when two people sit at the same table yet feel like strangers. You don't know if you're just tired or if it's over, and you don't know whether to continue or let go.

Fate will not make decisions for you, but it can help you see clearly: how deep the bond between you is, where the bottleneck in this relationship lies, and whether there is a possibility of resolving it.

What is the 'Marriage Palace' in the Eight Characters?

In the Eight Characters (Bazi), the Earthly Branch of the Day Pillar is called the 'Spouse Palace' or 'Marriage Palace,' and it is the key palace for judging the quality of marriage.

  • Day Master is strong and sits with noble people: marriage is stable, and the spouse is supportive
  • Day pillar clashes and penalties: Marriage is prone to friction and changes
  • Day Master enters the Tomb: Emotions are easily repressed, and communication encounters obstacles
  • Day Branch sitting on Peach Blossom: Strong emotions, but also need to guard against interference from a third party

When there are problems in a marriage, what signals are there in destiny/mingli?

The luck cycle year clashes with the day branch

This is the period when marriage is most likely to encounter a crisis. The Earthly Branch of the current year forms a six冲 (clash) with your Day Branch, causing disturbances in the spouse palace, and the relationship is prone to cracks. For example, if the Day Branch is Zi, and the current year is Wu (Zi-Wu clash), extra attention is needed to maintain marital stability.

Mix of officials and killers (for a woman's fate)

If a woman's destiny chart contains both the proper officer and the seven killers, her romantic relationships tend to become complicated, and she may be entangled in two relationships at the same time, leading to decreased marital stability.

The marital palace is vacant

When the Day Branch sits in Void, it indicates that the spouse palace has an element of 'emptiness.' The emotional relationship may appear complete on the surface but is hollow inside, or one party in the marriage may not be mentally invested in the marriage.

Hurting Officer meets Officer (for a female's destiny)

When a woman experiences the great luck of the 'Hurting Officer' while the annual luck encounters the 'Officer Star', she is likely to feel oppressed in her marriage, have conflicts in values with her husband, and experience cracks in the relationship.

The astrological signs of a marital crisis often appear simultaneously in multiple ways; a single sign should not be overinterpreted. Analyzing the two people's Bazi charts together can provide a more accurate judgment.

How to tell the depth of a couple's fate

The most accurate way to determine the depth of connection between two people is 'synastry'—analyzing the two people's Bazi together:

Day pillars compatible

When the day pillars of two people (especially the day stem or day branch) form a six-harmony or three-harmony, it is a sign of deep affinity, and the relationship is natural and stable.

Mutually serving as useful deities

The element missing in one person's fate chart happens to be abundant in the other person's chart, so the two complement each other, and their marriage is often more harmonious.

Spouse Palace Opposition

The two people's day pillars form a six clash, indicating a relatively shallow relationship. There will be more friction in their interactions, requiring more adjustment to maintain it.

Is there a possibility to resolve a marital crisis?

In terms of destiny, marital crises are usually cyclical—after the passing of certain years and the resolution of conflicts, the relationship often naturally warms up again. If a couple's astrological chart shows that the fate still exists, the following methods can help stabilize the marriage:

  • Reduce direct conflicts during a crisis and give the relationship some breathing space
  • Find the other person's 'useful god' through chart comparison, and learn to communicate in the way the other person needs.
  • Do not make major decisions such as divorce during a period of crisis; wait until the clash of the year's luck has passed before reassessing.
  • You can alleviate the impact on the marital palace by adjusting the Feng Shui of your residence.

If the compatibility chart shows that the fate between the two has run out, and there are already clear signals of separation in the life chart, then ending it in a peaceful manner is also a way of going with the flow.
