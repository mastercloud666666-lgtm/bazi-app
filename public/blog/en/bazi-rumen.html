Introduction to Bazi Fortune Telling: Complete Guide to the Four Pillars of Bazi

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-01 · Bazi Guide

Bazi fortune-telling, also known as "Four Pillars of Destiny," is a system of fate calculation that has been passed down in China for more than a thousand years. The "Four Pillars" refer to the year pillar, month pillar, day pillar, and hour pillar, each composed of one Heavenly Stem and one Earthly Branch, totaling eight characters, hence the name "Bazi."

What is Four Pillars of Destiny

The Eight Characters come from a person's year, month, day, and hour of birth, corresponding to the combination of four Heavenly Stems (Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, Gui) and four Earthly Branches (Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai). The Heavenly Stems represent yang aspects, while the Earthly Branches contain richer information about the Five Elements.

For example: Born at noon on June 15, 1990, the corresponding Eight Characters might be 'Geng-Wu year, Ren-Wu month, Geng-Shen day, Ren-Wu hour' — these eight characters are the code of this person's destiny.

The Relationship Between the Five Elements and the Eight Characters

The core of Bazi astrology is the Five Elements: Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth. Each Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch corresponds to specific Five Element attributes, and there are generating and controlling relationships among the Five Elements:

  • Mutual generation:Wood generates Fire, Fire generates Earth, Earth generates Metal, Metal generates Water, Water generates Wood
  • Mutual restraint:Wood controls Earth, Earth controls Water, Water controls Fire, Fire controls Metal, Metal controls Wood

A fortune teller determines a person's innate gifts and later fortune by analyzing the abundance or scarcity of the Five Elements in their destiny chart and how they generate, restrain, or transform each other.

Day Master: The Core of the Birth Chart

The most important element in the Eight Characters is the 'Day Master,' which is the Heavenly Stem of the Day Pillar and represents the person themselves. All judgments in fate analysis are centered around the Day Master—determining its strength and preferences, and then analyzing the power and position of the Ten Gods, including wealth, authority, resource, output, and harm.

Major Luck and Annual Luck

The Eight Characters themselves describe the innate destiny, while 'Major Luck' and 'Annual Luck' represent the rhythm of changes in acquired fortune. Major Luck changes every 10 years, Annual Luck rotates every year, and when Major Luck and Annual Luck are superimposed on the destiny chart, they become the source of a person's fortune each year.

💡 The same Eight Characters can have completely different development trajectories in different major fortune stages. The innate fate chart determines 'which cards are dealt,' while the major fortune and annual luck determine 'when to play the cards.'
