Annual Tai Sui: How to Resolve Offending Tai Sui

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-14 · Bazi Guide

Tai Sui, also known as 'Sui Jun', is a term used to represent the annual earthly branch of the year. There is a folk saying, 'Do not disturb the soil above Tai Sui's head,' which shows the important role of Tai Sui in fate and destiny culture. But what exactly is Tai Sui, what does offending Tai Sui mean, and whether it really needs to be appeased, this article will provide a detailed explanation for you.

What is Tai Sui?

Tai Sui refers to the earthly branch of the current year. The year 2025 is the Yi Si year, and Si is the Tai Sui of 2025; the year 2026 is the Bing Wu year, and Wu is the Tai Sui of 2026. When the earthly branches in a person's birth chart have a specific relationship with the Tai Sui of the current year, it is the source of 'offending Tai Sui'.

The Four Types of Offending Tai Sui

1. Confronting Tai Sui (Year Clash)

In a birth chart, if a certain earthly branch clashes with the Tai Sui of the current year, it is one of the strongest influences of Tai Sui conflicts. For example, in the year 2026, the Year of the Horse, those born with the Rat in their chart will experience a Rat-Horse clash. Years of clashing with Tai Sui are prone to changes: moving, changing jobs, relationship changes, and health fluctuations.

2. Punishment by Tai Sui (Yearly Punishment)

The Earthly Branches in the natal chart form a triple clash relationship with the Grand Duke Jupiter of the year. Years that clash with the Grand Duke often involve disputes, legal issues, stress, and hardships, so one needs to act with extra caution.

3. Breaking Tai Sui (Sui Po)

Forms a Six Break relationship with Tai Sui. The impact is relatively small, but it is easy to encounter repeated problems and plan disruption.

4. Harming Tai Sui (Yearly Harm)

Forms a six-harm relationship with Tai Sui. It often manifests as obstruction by petty people, secret depletion, and it is easy to encounter betrayal or misunderstanding.

💡 'Offending Tai Sui' does not necessarily mean that bad things will happen. If the year's Tai Sui is the useful god in a person's chart, even if one offends Tai Sui, it could still result in positive changes. The key is to look at the individual's chart and their useful god.

How to deal with the year of offending Tai Sui

From the perspective of destiny theory and practical life, in years that clash with Tai Sui, one can pay attention to:

  • Keep a low profile and reduce major risky decisions
  • Important matters (buying a house, surgery, marriage) should try to avoid the clash months.
  • Pay attention to your health and have regular check-ups
  • Do more good deeds and maintain a positive mindset; mental conditioning is more effective than any 'resolution'.

A Rational View on 'Transforming Tai Sui'

There are various 'Tai Sui appeasement' services on the market, which need to be approached rationally. The essence of destiny culture is to anticipate risks and be prepared, rather than relying on talismans or rituals. The truly effective 'resolution' is: adjusting your mindset, avoiding high-risk behaviors, and managing your health properly.
