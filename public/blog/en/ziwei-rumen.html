Introduction to Zi Wei Dou Shu: How Is It Different from Bazi Astrology?

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-24 · Bazi Guide

In traditional Chinese metaphysics, the Bazi (Four Pillars of Destiny) and Zi Wei Dou Shu are known as the two major mainstream systems. Many beginners cannot distinguish the differences between these two methods, nor do they know which one is more suitable for them. This article will provide a comprehensive comparison from the perspectives of core principles, applicable scenarios, and main characteristics.

What is Zi Wei Dou Shu

Zi Wei Dou Shu (abbreviated as Dou Shu) is a fortune-telling system that charts a twelve-palace horoscope based on one's birth year, month, day, and hour, using the fourteen main stars led by the Zi Wei Star as the core analytical tool. It is said to have been created by Chen Xi Yi of the Song Dynasty and is extremely popular in places like Taiwan and Hong Kong, and in recent years, its influence has been increasing in mainland China as well.

Unlike the Eight Characters, which use the heavenly stems and earthly branches as a symbolic system, Zi Wei Dou Shu uses twelve palaces (Life Palace, Wealth Palace, Career Palace, Marriage Palace, etc.) to host various main stars, analyzing different areas of life through the positions of the main stars in the palaces and the Four Transformations (Hua Lu, Hua Quan, Hua Ke, Hua Ji).

The Core Differences Between Zi Wei Dou Shu and Bazi Astrology

System Foundation

  • Eight characters: Using the Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches as symbols, the Five Elements generating, overcoming, restraining, and transforming each other serve as the logic for analysis
  • Zi Wei Dou Shu: Using the house system as the framework, the nature of the stars and the Four Transformations as the analytical logic

Information dimension

  • Eight characters: More skilled at analyzing the foundational personality, the strengths and weaknesses of the Five Elements, and the timing of major fortune cycles
  • Zi Wei Dou Shu: The house system provides a clearer division of various areas of life (relationships, wealth, career, health)

Differences in schools (or styles)

  • Eight charactersThe main schools include the Zi Ping method (traditional) and the Blind School (modern), among others. There are differences between schools, but the framework is unified.
  • Zi Wei Dou ShuThe Southern, Northern, and Central Plains schools differ significantly, and the same birth chart may be interpreted very differently under different schools.

Introduction to the Fourteen Major Stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu

The fourteen main stars are the core of Zi Wei Dou Shu, and each star has unique personality traits and areas it represents:

  • Ziwei Star: Leadership, nobility, desire for power, the star of emperors
  • Heavenly Secret Star: Wisdom, Planning, Change, Star of the Strategist
  • Sun StarBrightness, dedication, male benefactor, star of official nobility
  • Wu Qu Star: Wealth, fortitude, independence, the star of treasure
  • Tiān Tóng Star: Enjoyment, gentleness, popularity, lucky star
  • Lianzhen Star: Peach blossoms, schemes of power, the imprisoned star, the star of transformation
  • Tianfu Star: Conservative, accumulative, steady, the star of financial reserves
  • Taiyin Star: Gentle, feminine, wealth, yin star
  • Greedy Wolf Star: Desire, talent, romance, the star of desire
  • Giant Gate Star: Eloquence, right and wrong, deep thought, the dark shining star
  • Tianxiang Star: Assistance, honesty, seal and ribbon, seal star
  • Tianliang Star: Religion, medical care, elderly affinity, auspicious star
  • Seven Killings Star: Killing and conquest, independence, pioneering, brilliant star
  • Breaking Army Star: The star of breaking the old to establish the new, reform, and consumption; first break, then establish

What can Zi Wei Dou Shu analyze?

  • The pattern of lifeThe combination of stars in the three directions and four positions of the Life Palace determines the overall pattern of life.
  • Marriage based on loveThe stars in the Marriage Palace can be used to observe the characteristics of a spouse and the trends in the relationship.
  • Wealth modelThe combination of the Wealth Palace and the Property Palace reveals ways of making money
  • Career path: The Palace of Career shows suitable work fields and career level
  • Major Luck and Annual FortuneThe ten-year major cycle and the annual minor cycle can predict major events in different stages of life.

Who is Zi Wei Dou Shu suitable for learning

Zi Wei Dou Shu is suitable for those interested in the palace system and inclined toward holistic analysis. If you are more accustomed to the logically strict Five Elements generation and restriction system, Bazi might suit you better. The two are not mutually exclusive; many professional astrologers use both systems comprehensively to complement each other.

Both systems require an exact birth time (year, month, day, and hour). A difference of just one hour can result in a completely different horoscope. Therefore, confirming your exact birth time is the first step in learning or seeking a reading.
