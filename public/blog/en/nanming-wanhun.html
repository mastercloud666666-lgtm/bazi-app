Male Fate and Late Marriage: Which Bazi Patterns Tend to Marry Late

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-20 · Bazi Guide

The phenomenon of late marriage is becoming increasingly common in modern society. However, from the perspective of Bazi astrology, for some people, late marriage is not purely a matter of personal choice, but rather a matter of marital timing determined by the structure of their destiny chart. Understanding your own marriage timing can help you actively seize opportunities at the right moments.

Male's marriage star: Wealth star

In the destiny analysis of a man, the wealth star represents the wife (the main wealth star represents the legal wife, while the secondary wealth star can also represent a partner). The strength, position, and condition of the wealth star are key clues for judging the timing of marriage.

Characteristics of a destiny that easily leads to late marriage

1. The Wealth Star Enters the Treasury

When the wealth star (wife star) falls into the storage positions of Chen, Xu, Chou, or Wei, it is in a closed state, and marital fate is 'locked.' One needs to wait for the luck cycle or annual fortune that opens the wealth storage for the marital fate to manifest. Men with this type of fate often only meet a suitable partner after the age of 30, when the wealth storage is opened.

2. Wealth Star Void

When the Wealth Star falls within the void of the Day Pillar cycle, the Wife Star is empty and unreliable, making it difficult for relationships to take root. Only in the year when the void is filled can marital fate stabilize.

3. The Wealth Star is restricted by a combination

In the birth chart, the Wealth Star is restrained by other Earthly Branches and cannot flow freely. Men with this chart are not lacking in relationships, but their relationships often encounter changes at the crucial moment, making it difficult to enter marriage.

4. Officials and killings mixed (strong stamp without wealth)

The fate chart is dominated by the 印 star, while the wealth star is very weak or suppressed by the 印 star (印克财). The person focuses on career and studies, places relatively low importance on marriage, and is prone to postponing marriage actively.

💡 Marrying late does not mean poor marriage quality. Many men who marry late, after their financial resources are established, often find partners who are more compatible, and the quality of the marriage is actually higher.

Judging the timing of a man's marriage

The following situations are often signals that a man has encountered his destined partner:

  • Experiencing a prosperous period of wealth, the energy of the wife star is activated
  • The flow of the year breaks open the treasury, allowing the wife's star in the treasury to circulate.
  • The heavenly stem of the current year reveals the hidden wealth star in the natal chart
  • Annual fortune and the marriage palace (Day Branch) in harmonious combination

How to Proactively Create Opportunities in a Late Marriage Fate

Destiny provides a probability window, not a certainty. In years when marital fortune is better:

  • Actively expand your social circle and increase opportunities to interact with the opposite sex
  • Do not over-rely on work to distract yourself
  • Take every relationship with fate seriously
