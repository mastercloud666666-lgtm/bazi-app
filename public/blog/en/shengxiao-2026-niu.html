2026 Horoscope for those born in the Year of the Ox: How to Deal with the Clash Between Ox and Horse

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-24 · Bazi Guide

The year 2026 is the Bingwu year, with Bing Fire in the Heavenly Stem and Wu Fire in the Earthly Branch. Overall, the energy of fire is strong, affecting different zodiac signs in completely different ways. For those born in the Year of the Ox (Earthly Branch Chou, element Earth), their fortune in the Bingwu year needs to be analyzed based on the relationship between Chou and Wu.

Overall Horoscope for the Year of the Ox in 2026

In 2026, the Year of Bingwu, the Ox (Chou) and the Horse (Wu) form a Six Harms relationship. Six Harms, also known as Six Breaches, signify hidden depletion, guarding against petty people, and interpersonal conflicts. Compared to Six Clashes, the destructive power of Six Harms is more concealed and often comes from people you trust. In 2026, those born under the Ox sign will experience significant fluctuations in overall fortune and need to remain vigilant at all times to prevent hidden losses arising from interpersonal relationships.

Core Points for the Year 2026, Bingwu: The heavenly stem Bing fire is revealed, and the earthly branch Wu fire is strong. The overall social environment is full of competition and vitality; those who follow the trend will benefit, while those who go against it will face pressure.

Career and Wealth Luck

In the workplace, those born in the Year of the Ox are prone to encountering petty colleagues causing trouble behind their backs in 2026, or having collaborative projects secretly sabotaged. It is recommended to act low-key and reduce sharing unimplemented plans with others. In terms of finances, be cautious of loan disputes and contract traps, and be sure to carefully review important documents before signing.

Wealth Fortune Advice

  • Main source of wealth: Maintain a stable job income and avoid large speculative investments
  • Windfall Opportunities: Pay attention to investment opportunities in industries related to fire and wood
  • Note: Financial opportunities that arise this year need to be carefully identified, and be wary of verbal promises.

Emotions and Romantic Relationships

In terms of relationships, the 'Six Harms' have a notable impact on existing romantic relationships. Those born in the Year of the Ox who are married or in a stable partnership need to guard against interference from a third party and pay attention to handling relations between both sets of parents. For singles, the love fortune in 2026 is average; there is no need to force things, and it is more important to steadily accumulate personal qualities.

Relationship advice

  • Singles: Actively create social situations, opportunities for introductions by influential people are more frequent
  • Already has a partner: Focus on the quality of communication and avoid neglecting the relationship due to being busy with work.
  • Married individuals: Pay attention to your spouse's health and plan family goals together

Health Notice

When the Yin Earth is generated by the Yang Fire of Wu, the spleen, stomach, and digestive system are the health focus for those born in the Year of the Ox. With excessive fire and dry earth, digestive issues such as indigestion and gastritis are likely to occur. It is recommended to maintain a regular diet, eat less spicy and greasy food, and consume ingredients that strengthen the spleen and nourish the stomach.

Health preservation focus

  • Pay attention to the body parts corresponding to the Earth element in the Five Elements
  • In 2026, the fire element is strong; pay attention to cardiovascular health and blood pressure fluctuations.
  • Maintain a regular schedule and avoid excessive fatigue

Suggestions for Improving Luck and Resolving Problems

The most effective way to resolve the Six Harms of Chou and Wu is to introduce Si (Snake) or You (Rooster), forming a Si-You-Chou three-metal combination, so that metal can drain fire and alleviate the impact. You can also wear talismans of the Snake or Rooster zodiac. In addition, socialize more with friends who are not of the Horse zodiac and reduce the energy interference from those who are.

Specific operation

  • Carry corresponding Five Elements lucky charms with you
  • Avoid making major decisions in inauspicious directions.
  • Do more good deeds and accumulate virtues to increase positive energy.
