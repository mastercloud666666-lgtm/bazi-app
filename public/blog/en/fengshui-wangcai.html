Home Feng Shui Wealth Layout: Must-See Wealth-Attracting Feng Shui Guide for 2026

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-24 · Bazi Guide

Feng Shui and destiny are closely intertwined; a good home layout can help enhance the financial luck in one's life chart. The year 2026 is the Bingwu year, with strong fire energy, bringing specific considerations in Feng Shui arrangements. This article will provide a comprehensive analysis, covering the yearly wealth position, living room layout, wealth-enhancing ornaments, and Feng Shui taboos.

Feng Shui Characteristics of the Year 2026 Bingwu

In the year of Bingwu, the Heavenly Stem Bing Fire is strong in the south, and the Earthly Branch Wu Fire also governs the south, which means:

  • SouthIt is the prosperous direction for 2026, suitable for activity and brightness.
  • Southeast(Wood generates Fire) is the direction of vitality, suitable for arranging the wealth position.
  • North(Water overcomes Fire) This year's energy field is relatively weak, so it is not advisable to use important wealth positions.
  • The overall environment is filled with excessive fire energy, so it is advisable to appropriately introduce water elements (such as fish tanks or flowing water decorations) indoors to balance it.

Where is the wealth position in 2026?

In Feng Shui, wealth positions are divided into three categories:

Fixed Wealth Position (Eight House Wealth Position)

According to the orientation of the residence, it is usually determined at the diagonal position from the main door (the bright wealth position). The specific direction needs to be determined based on the orientation of the residence (facing south with the back to the north, facing west with the back to the east, etc.).

Annual Wealth Position

In the year 2026, the year of Bingwu, the wealth position is in the southeast (Wood). Placing wealth-enhancing ornaments in the southeast area of the home will have the best effect.

Feng Shui wealth position

According to the direction of the wealth star in a person's Bazi, it varies from person to person and requires professional feng shui analysis.

Living room feng shui layout for wealth enhancement

The living room is the core of a family's energy, and whether its layout is proper directly affects wealth luck:

Sofa orientation

  • The sofa backed against a solid wall represents having support and stable financial luck.
  • The sofa should not face away from the main door or windows to prevent the loss of wealth energy.
  • The central axis formed by the sofa and the TV should be as aligned with the direction of the door as possible.

Wealth Position Ornament (Recommended for 2026)

  • Southeast wealth position: green plants (lucky bamboo, pothos) + wooden ornaments
  • Southern Prosperity Position: Red elements (red ornaments, red cushions) enhance the fire energy, but should not be used excessively.
  • The living room should be kept tidy overall; disorder is the biggest enemy of wealth.

Color matching

For the living room in 2026, it is recommended to use wood and fire color schemes: green, beige, brown, and an appropriate amount of red, while avoiding large areas of black (water overcomes fire, suppressing this year's prosperous energy).

Bedroom Feng Shui and Wealth Luck

  • The head of the bed should not face directly towards the door to prevent wealth from being dispersed.
  • It is not advisable to hang a beam above the headboard, as it indicates pressure and obstruction.
  • The bedroom is not suitable for placing fish tanks or water features (excess moisture affects sleep and wealth).
  • Place a round table lamp on the bedside table to signify completeness.

The Feng Shui Relationship Between the Kitchen and the Treasury

In Feng Shui, the kitchen represents the treasury and is one of the most important areas for wealth prosperity:

  • Keep the stove clean; the accumulation of grease can deplete wealth luck.
  • The stove should not face the sink directly (water and fire conflict)
  • The refrigerator should not be left empty; keeping a certain amount of food stocked represents a plentiful treasury.
  • In 2026, small green plants can be placed in the southeast of the kitchen.

Recommended Wealth-Enhancing Ornaments for 2026

  • Copper ornament: Copper horse (matching the theme of the Year of the Horse in the Bingwu year), copper coin, copper gourd
  • Green plants: Lucky bamboo, money tree, and pachira (best placed in the wealth position)
  • crystal ball: Place a clear quartz crystal ball in the wealth area to gather energy and enhance prosperity
  • Fish tank: Keep 6 fish (5 gold, 1 black) or 9 goldfish, placing them in the southeast or north

Feng Shui Taboos in 2026

  • Mirror TaboosMirrors should not face the main door (wealth energy will be reflected away) and should not face the bed (affects health)
  • Aquarium TaboosAquariums should not be placed in the bedroom or next to the kitchen stove.
  • Sword and knife ornamentStrong negative energy, not suitable to be placed in a prominent position in the living room
  • Withered plant: Immediately clear away, the dead wood signifies decline
  • Pile of miscellaneous items: Do not pile miscellaneous items at the entrance or hallway, as it will obstruct the flow of energy.

Important reminder: Feng Shui is an auxiliary tool, while destiny analysis is fundamental. The same layout will have different effects on people with different Bazi. The most accurate Feng Shui advice should be customized based on an individual's Bazi chart.
