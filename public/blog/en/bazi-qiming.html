Naming by the Eight Characters: What is lacking in the Five Elements should be supplemented.

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-19 · Bazi Guide

In traditional Chinese culture, naming children has always been closely linked to the Eight Characters (Ba Zi) astrology. 'Adding water if lacking water, adding wood if lacking wood' is the first reaction of many parents when naming their child. But how effective is naming based on the Eight Characters? And how should it be approached scientifically?

The basic logic of naming based on the Eight Characters

The core idea of naming based on the Eight Characters: by analyzing a child's Eight Characters fate chart, identify the strengths and deficiencies of the Five Elements, and supplement the missing elements in the characters used in the name, so that the name complements the fate chart.

This idea originates from the philosophical concept of 'correspondence between heaven and humanity' — when a name is repeatedly called, its Five Elements energy continuously affects the person.

The method of determining the Five Elements of a name

There are usually two ways to determine the Five Elements affiliation of characters:

  • Meaning and Five Elements:The meaning of characters directly corresponds to one of the Five Elements, for example, '森', '林', '木' belong to Wood, and '炎', '煌' belong to Fire.
  • Character form Five Elements:The radicals of Chinese characters correspond to the Five Elements, such as '氵' for Water, the '火' radical for Fire, and the '金' radical for Metal.
  • Tone and the Five Elements:Some schools determine the Five Elements based on the number of strokes and tones (such as the Three Talents and Five Grids in name studies)

💡 Different schools of fate and fortune have slightly different criteria for determining the Five Elements in characters, which is also one of the reasons why 'Bazi naming' is controversial. It is recommended to refer to multiple methods for a comprehensive judgment, rather than relying entirely on a single standard.

The practical function of naming based on the Eight Characters

There are different views in the field of destiny studies regarding the actual impact of names on fortune:

  • Proponents believe that the Five Elements of a name interact with one's fate chart, and that long-term cumulative effects do exist.
  • Conservatives believe that a name is just an aid, and the innate structure of one's fate is the determining factor.
  • Pragmatists believe that the greatest value of a good name lies in being easy to remember, sounding pleasant, and having a beautiful meaning, which has a positive effect on a child's psychological development.

Practical advice for naming

Whether or not you fully believe in naming based on fate, the following suggestions are practically valuable:

  • Prioritize choosing characters that sound bright and have positive meanings
  • Avoid uncommon characters (they affect daily life and make handling documents troublesome)
  • The meaning of the characters must withstand combined scrutiny (the full name when read together does not cause ambiguity)
  • On the basis of the above, make supplements with reference to the Five Elements of the natal chart.
