Can feelings be salvaged: Fate tells you which relationships are worth fighting for and which should be let go

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

After breaking up, the same question keeps repeating in my mind: Is it still possible?

When it comes to salvaging a relationship, what matters more than effort is judgment—judging whether the fate is truly over and whether now is the right moment to take action. Fate science can help you make this judgment.

The relationship has 'scattered'; what signals are there in fate?

In Bazi, the following situations indicate that the energy of this relationship has been exhausted:

  • The couple star enters the tomb or goes out of play: The corresponding spouse star in the natal chart has already been severely restrained or depleted, and the basic energy for the relationship is insufficient
  • The Day Branch is completely opposed.: The Spouse Palace continues to be under opposition, and it is unable to stabilize, indicating that the foundation of the relationship between the two has already weakened.
  • There are no points of mutual generation in the combined chart of the two people.There is almost no supportive energy between the two destinies; being together instead leads to mutual exhaustion.
  • The Divorce Star appears in both individuals' annual fortunes.This year has been a turning point of fate for both of them, and mutual signals are more meaningful than one-way ones.

Fate is still there, signals that reconciliation is possible

  • The Day Pillars or the marital stars of the two people still have mutual attraction (for example, there is a Six Harmony or Three Harmony relationship).
  • The person who is breaking up is experiencing fortunate luck in relationships, and their emotional needs are increasing.
  • In your current year, signals of 'combined day branch' or 'strong peach blossom' appear, and the other person is also at the same stage in their relationship.
  • The two charts have a 'clamping arch' relationship—the information in the fate chart tightly connects the two people.

The composite astrological conditions require the joint analysis of both people's Bazi; looking at your own chart alone can only reveal half of the picture. A combined chart is the most accurate way to judge the possibility of a reunion.

When is the most effective time to take action

Even if fate is still present, if the timing is wrong, actions may backfire. The following are high-probability timing points for reconciliation actions:

The other person's peach blossom year

The other party encounters the 'peach blossom' character in their fortune year, activating their emotional needs. Contacting them at this time is likely to resonate.

Your harmonious divine year

The earthly branch of the year combines with your day branch, activating the spouse palace, increasing emotional energy, making actions more likely to yield results.

Before and after the major luck cycle change

Both of their major fortunes are simultaneously in a period of change, the old patterns are loosening, and the relationship has the potential to be redefined.

What does fate theory say when reconciliation is ineffective?

If after analysis it is found that: the combined chart shows that the fate has dissipated, and neither party's annual fortune shows any sign of emotional recovery—then trying to win them back is likely just exhausting your own energy.

Fortune-telling is not about making you 'accept your fate,' but about helping you focus your energy on places where it is truly likely to flourish. Just because a relationship has ended doesn't mean your luck in love is over—it only means that chapter has ended, and the next one is waiting for the right moment to appear.

Understand when your next prosperous year in relationships will be, and save your energy for the person who truly belongs to you; this is more worthwhile than clinging to a bond that has already faded.
