Feeling like you haven't achieved anything at 40: Astrology tells you how to navigate the second half of life

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

I'm 40 years old. Looking back over these years, it seems like I have tried hard and worked hard, but I don't know why, I always feel that something is wrong — I haven't achieved what I imagined, I can't get excited about my current life, and I have no expectations for the future.

This is not a feeling unique to you, nor does it mean you have failed. In destiny studies, there is a set of segmented logic for life to help you understand: where you are, and where the next stage is.

The division of life stages according to fate theory

The major luck in the Eight Characters changes every ten years, and a person's life can be divided into several major stages:

  • Ages 1-20 (Period of 印运/比劫运): Accumulation period, learning, establishing identity
  • Ages 20-40 (Food and Injury/Wealth Luck)Development period, showing talent, building a career and family
  • Ages 40-60 (official seal luck or wealth-official luck): During the harvest period, luck often truly matures at this stage
  • After 60 years old: Period of accumulation, enjoy the fruits of your efforts

Many people feel confused at the age of 40 because the previous major luck cycle (related to food and wealth) has ended, and they are in the transition period where the new major luck cycle (related to career and reputation) has just begun—the old pattern has ended, but the new pattern has not yet stabilized, and this gap period is when it is easiest to feel lost.

The rhythm of life varies from person to person. Some people's destinies accumulate steadily in the first half of life and only truly burst forth in the second half. Feeling lost at 40 may actually be the 'period of gathering strength' before the second half begins.

Why do some people feel unaccomplished at 40?

In the first half of life, I took a path that didn't suit me

People with an overly strong influence of the planet Jupiter are easily guided by the expectations of their parents and society, taking the 'right' path that doesn't suit them. It's only in middle age that they begin to feel a strong sense of emptiness—this is not a failure, but rather, after accumulating enough experience, the true self begins to awaken.

After the period of the Output Star luck is completed, the stage when the Peer Star is strong

The luck period with abundant food and injury is the most creative stage in life. After it ends, one enters a relatively quiet period, often feeling 'not as active as before.' But this stage is a time to accumulate inner strength, not the end.

The fate chart is naturally the type that develops later.

Some life charts belong to the 'late bloomer' pattern — the breakout point of the official and seal stars in the life chart occurs between the ages of 40 and 60. The buildup in the first half of life is precisely to support the performance in the second half of life.

After 40, what things are worth doing

Find the direction that truly belongs to yourself

After the age of 40, many people finally have the leverage to say, 'I don't want to wrong myself anymore.' By analyzing the Eight Characters (Ba Zi), you can identify the true useful god and traits in your destiny chart, and focus your energy on directions that are truly suitable for you.

Re-establish connection

The core of a midlife crisis is often a disconnection from oneself. Find what makes you feel 'alive'—it doesn’t have to be something that makes money, just something that makes you feel 'right'.

Clearly see the upcoming great fortune

Calculate the major luck and yearly fortune after the age of 40 to see what kind of luck the next ten years will bring. If it is official and seal luck, this is the stage when career and life recognition truly arrives; if it is wealth luck, it is a period for accumulating wealth and resources. Understanding this can help you focus your energy in the right places.

The ultimate answer of fate theory to midlife crisis

Midlife crisis, the interpretation in terms of fate is: you are at the transition point between the first and second halves of your life; the old pattern has completed its mission, and a new pattern is taking shape.

The confusion at this stage is not a sign of failure, but a precursor to an impending change. Seeing clearly where you are and where to go next is more valuable than staying in self-blame for 'achieving nothing'.
