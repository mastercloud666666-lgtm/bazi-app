What to Do After a Breakup: Eight-Character Astrology Tells You When Your Love Life Will Turn Around

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

Heartbroken, the hardest part is not the separation itself, but not knowing 'what will happen next'—not knowing if it can be salvaged, not knowing when the next relationship will come, not knowing if this is just how life is meant to be for oneself.

Fate studies are not meant to make you accept your destiny, but to help you see the rhythm of your fortune and do the right things at the right time.

Is heartbreak destined?

In Bazi astrology, the ups and downs of relationships are closely related to the major luck cycles and annual luck. Some people are very likely to experience emotional setbacks during a certain major luck cycle or annual luck—not because 'you are not good enough,' but because the energy in the chart is unfavorable for emotional stability at this stage.

Common signs of emotional turmoil include:

  • In the decade luck or annual luck, one follows the 'Hurting Officer' luckThe 'Hurting Officer' star signifies rebellion and opposition to authority. For women, it can easily harm the husband and affect romantic relationships; for men, it leads to emotional impulsiveness and can cause friction with their partner.
  • Day Branch is being opposedThe Day Branch represents the spouse palace. When the annual luck forms a six冲 (six clash) with the Day Branch, the stability of the relationship is greatly reduced.
  • The marital palace encounters punishment and combinationWhen the spouse palace is affected by both punishment and combination, the emotional relationship becomes complicated, making it easy for a third party to appear or for separation to occur.

A strong 'Hurting Officer' in the annual chart and conflict in the Day Branch are two major signals that indicate the highest likelihood of emotional problems. You can confirm your current stage of fortune by checking your chart.

After a breakup, when will my luck in love improve?

Fortunes have lows and then recover. The key to judging a turning point in a relationship is to see when the 'Peach Blossom Star' and 'Harmony Deity' enter during the major and annual fortunes:

Peach Blossom Year

When the earthly branch of the current year corresponds to the 'peach blossom' character in your natal chart (those with the Yin, Wu, Xu chart encountering a Mao year, and those with the Shen, Zi, Chen chart encountering a You year), your luck with the opposite sex naturally improves, and opportunities for new relationships increase.

Annual Luck Combining with Day Branch

The annual luck forms a harmonious combination with your Day Branch (Zi-Chou, Yin-Hai, Mao-Xu, Chen-You, Si-Shen, Wu-Wei), opening up the spouse palace, making it easier for your relationship to have new developments.

Major luck transition period

The three years before and after the major luck cycle changes are the period when all aspects of life change the most, and the pattern of relationships may also be reshuffled.

After breaking up, can it be reconciled?

In destiny studies, there are signals of a 'reunion.' If you and your ex:

  • The couple stars in each person's Bazi still correspond to each other
  • The current major fortune is favorable for relationships, rather than bringing misfortune to them.
  • In the flowing year, 'He Shen' appears and shines on two people at the same time

So the possibility of reconciliation exists. But if a relationship has already reached its 'dissolution' according to one's fate chart, trying hard to salvage it will consume a lot of energy, and the final result will still be the same.

Understanding this is not for giving up, but for focusing energy on directions that are truly worth investing in.

During a breakup, what should be done from the perspective of fate/fortune?

1. First, stabilize the current situation

When experiencing strong emotional fluctuations, it is not suitable to make major decisions (whether to reconcile or contact the other party). Give yourself some time and wait until your emotions stabilize before taking action. This aligns with the principle in fate studies that 'during a low fortune period, one should not force things.'

2. Understand the general trends of your emotions

By analyzing the Eight Characters chart, you can determine which major fortune and annual fortune you are currently in and whether they are helpful or hindering to your relationship. If the overall fortune is unfavorable for love, rather than forcing the relationship, it is better to focus on personal growth and wait for the fortune to improve.

3. Use destiny studies to find the right timing for relationships

Different people have different points when their romantic luck improves. Some meet new opportunities in their 'peach blossom year,' while others need to wait for a major fortune change. Casting an annual fortune chart can help you see which time period is worth actively pursuing.
