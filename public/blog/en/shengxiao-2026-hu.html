The year 2026, the Bingwu Year, is a highlight year for those born in the Year of the Tiger. The nurturing relationship of Wood generating Fire further enhances this, so the overall fortune of Tiger-born individuals in 2026 will be on the rise, with abundant energy and assistance from benefactors. It is a rare good year in the past three years.

Core Points for the Year 2026, Bingwu: The heavenly stem Bing fire is revealed, and the earthly branch Wu fire is strong. The overall social environment is full of competition and vitality; those who follow the trend will benefit, while those who go against it will face pressure.

Career and Wealth Luck

Career will be the biggest highlight for those born in the Year of the Tiger in 2026. For professionals with promotion opportunities, it is highly likely that their wishes will be fulfilled this year; for Tigers planning to start a business, taking advantage of the gathering power of the 'Three Harmonies Fire' arrangement, this year is an excellent time to launch new projects. Emotions and Romantic Relationships

In terms of emotions, those born in the Year of the Tiger will have a strong romantic luck in 2026, with an active social circle and a greatly increased chance of meeting someone they fancy. Singles are likely to experience a breakthrough in their love life in the first half of the year; those who are already in a relationship will see their emotions heat up, making it suitable to plan the future together, and those considering marriage or having children can prioritize this year.

Relationship advice
