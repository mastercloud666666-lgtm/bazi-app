Feeling lost in life: Astrology helps you find the direction you’ve been unable to find

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

You have changed several jobs and tried a few directions, but still can't find that feeling of 'rightness.' Or even though your conditions are not bad, you just can't get excited about the future and don't know what you really want.

The deeper reason for confusion is sometimes not a lack of effort, but that the direction itself does not match one's inherent characteristics. Finding the right direction is more important than running hard.

How to determine a person's innate talents through astrology

In the Bazi, each person's fate structure determines the areas of ability they are naturally good at:

God of Food Flourishing

Highly creative, with good aesthetics and talent, suitable for art, catering, handicrafts, and consulting, monetizing through their own skills. People with the 'God of Food' pattern are usually not suitable for purely managerial work; giving them space to express themselves freely is the only way they can achieve results.

Official seal generates

Clear in logic, authoritative, skilled in management and execution, suitable for positions within institutions, corporate management, or academic research, and for work environments that require structure and standards.

Hurting Officer Pattern

Quick-thinking, highly creative, dislikes being constrained, suitable for entrepreneurship, media, consulting, and technological research and development. People with the 'Shang Guan' configuration often feel stifled in traditional companies and are more suited to more autonomous forms of work.

Wealth Structure

Strong execution, pragmatic, sensitive to money and resources, suitable for business, sales, investment, and management roles, suitable for jobs where results can be seen directly.

Your natal chart tells you what you are good at, while the luck cycles and annual influences tell you when to exert effort. Only by combining the two can you find the 'right direction and the right timing'.

Why can't I find the feeling even after switching directions several times?

There are several common astrological reasons:

  • Experiencing a major luck period related to siblings and peersAt this stage, it is easy to be influenced by others, following their choices rather than following the characteristics of your own destiny, so naturally, you can't find a sense of direction.
  • Hurting Officer Meets OfficerStrongly rejecting the feeling of being controlled internally, but haven't yet found an environment where I can act independently, stuck in the awkward stage where neither side feels right.
  • Overly strong Printing StarRelying on external approval, with direction overly influenced by parents and elders, following a path that others consider 'good' rather than one that truly suits oneself.

When will I be able to find a clear direction?

In terms of fate, the following times are often turning points when 'direction is clear':

  • Luck cycle change: A new luck cycle usually brings new perspectives and opportunities, and the old confusion often naturally resolves after the change of fortune.
  • Enter the market using the year's favorable deity: In the year, encountering the useful element (the core required Five Element) in one’s fate chart will suddenly enhance the sense of direction
  • Year of the God of Food or Hurting Officer: During this kind of year, creativity and self-expression energy are strong, often serving as a point to find new directions

What does destiny suggest to do when feeling confused?

  • Stop comparing—other people's 'success' paths may not fit your destiny, and comparing will only deepen confusion.
  • Find your own destiny traits through charting your fate, starting from 'what am I naturally good at' rather than 'what makes the most money'
  • Confirm the attributes of the current luck cycle. If it is an accumulation period (Yin luck), recharge first rather than sprint; if it is a period of exertion (wealth luck/official luck), take bold action.
  • Confusion itself is also a kind of signal: the old direction no longer fits, the new direction has not yet taken shape, patiently wait for the change in fortune.
