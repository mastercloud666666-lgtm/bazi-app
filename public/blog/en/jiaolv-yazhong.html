Excessive Anxiety and Stress: Astrology Tells You Why You Are More Prone to Anxiety Than Others

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-27 · Bazi Guide

With the same workload, others handle it with ease, while you are anxious to the point of insomnia. Faced with the same challenges, others find it exciting, while you feel overwhelmed.

This may not necessarily be your problem; it could just be that your destiny determines a different 'stress tolerance threshold' for you. Understanding this is the key to finding the stress-relief method that truly suits you.

Which features in the Eight Characters indicate a tendency to anxiety

In fate analysis, a person's mental resilience is closely related to the strength of the Day Master, the condition of the Seal Star, and the auspicious and inauspicious influences in their chart.

Weak Day Master, lacking the Seal Star

The Day Master represents the self, and the Seal Star represents a sense of security and support. People with a weak Day Master and lacking the Seal Star have insufficient inner stability, and external changes can easily trigger anxiety. They need more certainty to feel at ease.

Flourishing Hurting Officer

Shangguan represents active thinking and an obsession with perfection. It is easy to be overly concerned with details, and a nonstop mind is an important source of anxiety. People with a strong Shangguan often have extremely high self-demands, which in turn become the greatest source of pressure.

When the Seven Killings star is strong and uncontrolled

Seven Killings represent pressure, competition, and external challenges. If Seven Killings is strong but the fate chart cannot restrain it, it will make a person feel a long-term sense of being 'chased,' which can easily cause problems with sleep and emotions.

The Five Elements in the destiny chart are imbalanced

An excessive or insufficient presence of a certain element in the Five Elements can affect the corresponding emotions and organs. For example, excessive Wood (excessive liver Wood) can lead to irritability and anger; weak Water (weak kidney Water) can lead to fear and anxiety; weak Fire (weak heart Fire) can lead to low mood and lack of motivation.

Understanding the balance of the Five Elements in your own fate chart allows you to choose stress relief methods more specifically—not everyone is suited to the same approach.

Why are some stages particularly anxious?

Even if the basic fate chart is relatively good, at certain stages of fortune, the sense of anxiety will also increase significantly:

  • Entering the Seven Killings luck cycle: External pressure increases, competition is fierce, life is full of uncertainties, and it is easy to fall into continuous tension
  • The annual luck is a mix of authority and killing starsPressure from work and interpersonal relationships erupts at the same time, and coping ability is insufficient
  • Luck Cycle Change PeriodThe pattern of fortune is shifting, inner feelings of uncertainty are strong, and anxiety often reaches its peak during this phase.

Different destinies are suited to different ways of relieving stress

A person whose Day Master is weak and lacks support

What is needed most is a sense of 'stability.' Establishing a regular routine, reducing information intake, and finding trustworthy people to talk to are more effective than any motivational method.

A person with a strong 'Hurting Officer'

It is necessary to release excess mental energy. Creative activities such as writing, painting, music, and handicrafts are the best outlets; meditation often works poorly for people with a strong 'Hurting Officer' (a term in Chinese astrology), as an overactive mind can lead to more anxiety.

A person with a strong 'Seven Killings'

A sense of 'control' is needed. Break problems down into executable small steps, and give yourself confirmation after completing each step; this can effectively relieve the anxiety brought by 'Seven Killings.' Exercise is the best stress-relief outlet for those with a strong 'Seven Killings'.

When will the feeling of anxiety naturally decrease?

If you are currently going through a challenging luck cycle (Seven Killings, Official Killing), anxiety will periodically appear during this cycle. As the luck cycle changes, the pattern of stress will also change.

At the same time, if there is an appearance of the 'Yin star' (benefactors, supporting forces) in the flow of the year, the external support system will be strengthened, and the sense of anxiety will also significantly decrease. Understanding this rhythm can help you face the current high-pressure phase with a calmer mindset.
