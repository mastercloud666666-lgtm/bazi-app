Strength of the Day Master: How to Determine Whether the Bazi Is Strong or Weak

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-12 · Bazi Guide

In Bazi astrology, the 'strength or weakness of the Day Master' is the starting point for all analysis. Without understanding whether the Day Master is strong or weak, one cannot determine the favorable or unfavorable elements, nor can one accurately analyze wealth, marriage, and career.

Why is the strength of the Day Master so important?

The Day Master represents the person whose fate is being analyzed. Only if the Day Master is strong enough can they have the ability to control wealth and authority and carry favorable luck; if the Day Master is too weak, encountering wealth and authority instead becomes a pressure, and good fortune cannot be sustained. Therefore, determining strength or weakness is the fundamental framework of fate analysis.

Three dimensions for determining the strength of the Day Master

1. Monthly Order (Most Important)

The monthly branch represents the season of birth and is the place where the Five Elements are in charge. If the Five Element of the monthly branch shares the same energy with or generates the element of the day stem, the day master receives its mandate, significantly increasing its strength.

  • Jia and Yi wood are born in the months of Yin and Mao (spring): wood is in its season, and the day master is strong.
  • Bing and Ding fire are born in the months of Si and Wu (summer): fire is in its season, and the day master is strong
  • Geng and Xin Metal are born in the months of Shen and You (Autumn): Metal is in season, and the day master is strong.
  • Ren and Gui Water are born in the Hai and Zi months (winter): Water is in season, and the Day Master is strong.

2. Receiving Earth (Hidden Stems of the Earth Branches)

Apart from the monthly branch, if the year branch, day branch, or hour branch contains the same element as the day master (having a common root), it will also strengthen the power of the day master. The day branch is the foundation under the feet of the life master, and a common root in the day branch is particularly important.

3. Receiving Help (Heavenly Stem Exposed)

If in the heavenly stems of the year, month, or hour, there appears a heavenly stem of the same element as the Day Master (Peer/Companion) or an element that supports the Day Master (Resource star), the power of the Day Master is strengthened; if there appears an element that restrains or depletes the Day Master (Wealth, Officer, or Output stars), the power of the Day Master is consumed.

💡 The monthly branch carries the most weight, accounting for more than 50% of the judgment of prosperity and decline. Next is whether the day branch has a corresponding root, followed by the support from the heavenly stem. Only by comprehensively weighing these three dimensions can an accurate conclusion be reached.

Special structure: From pattern

When the day master is extremely weak and there is no supportive force in the chart, and the entire chart's Five Elements are highly concentrated in one direction, it may form a 'cong pattern'—at this time, instead, one follows the strong deity, no longer using the support of the day master as the useful god. Judging a cong pattern requires extreme caution and is one of the most complex structures in destiny analysis.

The Influence of Strength and Weakness on the Selection of Useful Deities

  • If the Day Master is strong: favoring release and consumption (food and output) or control and restraint (wealth and officials)
  • Day Master is weak: prefers support and assistance (Resource stars, Peer stars)
  • Balanced Day Master: Takes adjustment of seasons and structure as the main considerations
