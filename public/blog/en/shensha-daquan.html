Complete Guide to Auspicious Stars: Detailed Explanation of Benefactors, Wenchang Star, Traveling Horse, and Peach Blossom

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-10 · Bazi Guide

Shensha is an auxiliary analysis system in Bazi astrology that, through specific Gan-Zhi calculation rules, identifies special symbols in a person's chart and reveals the innate tendencies of the individual in areas such as interpersonal benefactors, learning ability, travel opportunities, and romantic relationships.

Tianyi Nobleman: The Most Important Benefactor Star in One's Fate

Tianyi Nobleman is one of the most important deities in the Eight Characters astrology, representing that the person is likely to receive selfless help and support from others at critical moments. Those who have the Tianyi Nobleman are more likely to turn misfortune into fortune.

Calculation method: Use the day's Heavenly Stem to check the corresponding Earthly Branch of the Heavenly Nobleman (for example, for day masters of Jia, Wu, or Geng, the noblemen are Chou and Wei). When the corresponding Earthly Branch appears in the natal chart or major/fate cycles, the power of the nobleman is activated.

Wenchang Nobleman: The Star of Learning and Talent

The Wenchang nobleman represents intelligence, learning ability, and literary and artistic temperament. People with Wenchang in their destiny usually have high learning efficiency and strong expressive ability, making them suitable for careers in writing, education, media, and other fields.

Calculation: Calculate based on the day stem. For example, if the day master is Jia, the Wen Chang is in Si; if the day master is Yi, the Wen Chang is in Wu, and so on.

Travel Horse Star: The Star of Travel and Change

The 'Yi Ma' star represents movement, change, business trips, and overseas opportunities. People with 'Yi Ma' in their destiny often experience frequent relocations and travel, or engage in work that requires a lot of commuting.

  • When the traveling star is prosperous and usable: traveling brings opportunities, and development in other places goes more smoothly.
  • Yi Ma is affected: frequent changes, more separations than gatherings
  • Traveling Horse Enters Wealth: Gaining wealth due to traveling or relocating

💡 The key of the Traveling Horse star is not just in 'movement', but in 'moving with direction'. When experiencing the Traveling Horse luck cycle, it is a good time to adjust your place of residence or expand business in other locations.

Peach Blossom Star: The Star of Romance and Popularity

Peach blossom star represents charm, attraction to the opposite sex, and love life. Those with peach blossom in their fate usually have an attractive appearance, are socially active, and have a rich love life.

Peach blossoms divided into 'Salty Pool Peach Blossoms' and 'Peach Blossoms Within the Wall':

  • Peach blossom in the year branch or day branch (peach blossom within the walls): Emotions are relatively stable, and peach blossoms mostly help with genuine relationships.
  • Hour Branch Peach Blossom: Emotional life remains active in later years
  • Fortune year meets romance: that year's romantic opportunities increase
