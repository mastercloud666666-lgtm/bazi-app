How to Supplement Metal in the Five Elements? A Complete Guide from Bazi Astrology to Daily Luck Improvement

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-24 · Bazi Guide

In Bazi astrology, a deficiency of Metal in the Five Elements is a relatively common characteristic of a birth chart. Metal represents willpower, decisiveness, a sense of justice, and the ability to restrain oneself. Those lacking Metal often need to make extra efforts in these areas. This article will approach from the perspective of astrology to help you fully understand the impacts of a Metal deficiency in the Five Elements and ways to remedy it.

What does it mean to lack metal in the Five Elements?

A deficiency of Metal in the Five Elements refers to a situation where, in a person's Four Pillars of Destiny (year pillar, month pillar, day pillar, hour pillar), the characters in the heavenly stems and earthly branches that represent Metal (Geng, Xin, Shen, You) are very few or completely absent. In the Five Elements, Metal corresponds to the West and the autumn season, and its nature is contracting, austere, and decisive.

It should be noted that a lack of metal is not only judged by the literal amount of metal, but also needs to be analyzed in combination with the useful god—sometimes there is metal in the Eight Characters, but if the metal is excessively restrained, the actual effect is equivalent to a lack of metal.

Personality Traits of Those Lacking Metal in the Five Elements

People who lack metal (in terms of the Five Elements) usually have the following personality traits:

  • Lack of decisivenessTends to be indecisive when facing important decisions, overanalyzing and delaying action.
  • Weak willpowerIt is easy to give up halfway when doing something; the perseverance to see things through to the end needs to be deliberately cultivated.
  • Blurred sense of boundaries: Not good at saying 'no' to others, easily having one's time and energy taken up by others
  • A relatively weak sense of justice: The sense of identification with rules and order is not as strong as that of Jin Wang.

Important Note: The personality traits of those lacking metal in the Five Elements are tendencies and not absolute. Other factors in the natal chart (such as the strength of the day master and the configuration of useful gods) also have a significant impact on personality, so a conclusion cannot be drawn based solely on the lack of metal.

Body signals of a deficiency in metal in the Five Elements

In the correspondence between traditional Chinese medicine and fate analysis, metal corresponds to the lungs, large intestine, skin, and nose. Those who lack metal may have potential health risks in the following areas:

  • Respiratory system: prone to rhinitis, asthma, and bronchitis
  • Skin problems: sensitive skin, prone to allergies, slow wound healing
  • Large intestine function: constipation or slower bowel movements

Method One to Add Metal: Color and Direction

Color is the most convenient way to regulate the Five Elements:

  • Favorable colorsWhite, beige, gold, silver
  • Favorable direction: West (The West corresponds to Metal; working or living in the western position helps to attract the energy of Metal)
  • Daily applications: White underwear, gold accessories, desktop ornaments in white or gold

Method Two to Increase Gold: Choosing Profession and Environment

Choose industries related to the metal element, so you can continuously replenish metal energy in your work:

  • Finance, banking, securities, insurance (metal generates water, money)
  • Law, Judiciary, Supervision (Justice of the money owner, Rules)
  • Medical devices, surgery (especially lucrative instruments)
  • Metal manufacturing, machinery, automobiles
  • Military and police, sports competitions (strict sponsors, competition)

Method Three to Nourish Gold: Dietary Adjustment

In traditional Chinese dietary therapy, spicy foods enter the lungs, and white foods nourish the metal element.

  • white food: White radish, lily, white fungus, lotus root, pear, white sesame
  • Spicy food(appropriate amount): green onions, ginger, garlic, white pepper
  • Foods that nourish the lungsLily Congee, White Fungus and Lotus Seed Sweet Soup, Snow Pear Stewed with Rock Sugar

Method Four for Enhancing Gold: Auspicious Items and Decorations

  • White crystal (clear quartz, moonstone): enhances the energy of metal
  • Metal ornaments: copper bells, copper wind chimes, silverware
  • Auspicious animals for those belonging to the Metal Zodiac: Monkey (Shen Metal), Rooster (You Metal)

Precautions for Gold Supplementation

Many people mistakenly think that if they lack metal, they need to wear a lot of gold jewelry or buy gold, but in fact:

  • The key to supplementing gold lies in consistency, rather than a large intake at one time.
  • White (platinum, silver) is closer to the 'metal' in terms of fate significance than gold.
  • Spiritual supplementation (cultivating decisiveness and establishing a sense of rules) is more fundamental than material supplementation.
  • If metal is the unfavorable element in the natal chart, one should not add metal and should seek the judgment of a professional fortune-teller.

How to accurately determine if there is a real deficiency of metal

Judging the missing elements of the Five Elements on your own has certain limitations; it is recommended to confirm through a professional Bazi analysis:

  1. Count the number of characters representing Metal in the Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches (Geng, Xin, Shen, You)
  2. Analyze the strength or weakness of metal (whether it is in season or has a favorable position)
  3. Determine whether metal is the useful deity or the taboo deity based on the strength of the day master.
  4. Comprehensively assess whether additional funds are needed and the extent of the supplementation
