Bazi Health: Which Physical Problems Correspond to Imbalances in the Five Elements

Yunzi Destiny · 2026-03-07 · Bazi Guide

Traditional Chinese medicine and Bazi astrology share the same roots and origin, both based on the theory of the Five Elements. The strength or imbalance of the Five Elements in Bazi often corresponds to a person's congenital health tendencies and weak areas of the body. Understanding these can help with early prevention and targeted care.

The Correspondence Between the Five Elements and the Zang-Fu Organs

The basic correspondence between the Five Elements and human organs:

  • Wood → Liver and Gallbladder:Excretory function, emotional management, muscles, bones, and eyes
  • Fire → Heart Small Intestine:Circulatory system, cardiovascular and cerebrovascular, tongue and consciousness
  • Earth → Spleen and Stomach:Digestive system, limb muscles, thinking and emotions
  • Metal → Lung Large Intestine:Respiratory system, skin, nose, and courage
  • Water → Kidney Bladder:Urinary and reproductive, bone marrow and spine, ear and willpower

Which of the Five Elements is missing or too weak in the Eight Characters

When a certain element is very scarce or even completely absent in the Eight Characters chart, the corresponding organ system is relatively weak. For example:

  • Lacking water in the life chart or water extremely weak: kidney deficiency, urinary problems, soreness in the waist and legs, lack of energy
  • Lack of wood in the life chart or extremely weak wood: liver and gallbladder problems, declining vision, depressed emotions, inflexible tendons and bones
  • Lack of fire or weak fire in the natal chart: heart stress, poor circulation, cold hands and feet, low energy
  • Lack of metal or weak metal in the birth chart: sensitive lungs, skin problems, weak intestinal function
  • Birth chart lacks Earth or Earth is weak: indigestion, weak spleen and stomach, fluctuating appetite

💡 Having an 'excessive' presence of certain elements in your life chart also carries health risks. Excessive Fire can lead to inflammation and high blood pressure; excessive Water can cause edema and damp-cold conditions; excessive Wood can result in emotional agitation and excessive liver heat. Balance is the key to health.

The impact of the grand luck and annual luck on health

Even if the Five Elements in one's destiny chart are relatively balanced, the major luck cycles and annual influences will bring periodic changes in the strength of the Five Elements, affecting the health condition during the corresponding periods. For example, during a Metal luck cycle, special attention should be paid to the lungs; during a Water luck cycle, care should be taken for the kidneys and bladder.

Health prevention in years when the favorable element is weak

When the annual clash affects the life chart's useful god, not only will career and wealth be impacted, but health is also likely to experience problems. It usually manifests as a decline in immunity, recurrence of old illnesses, or accidental injuries. In such years, it is even more necessary to pay attention to balancing work and rest, as well as regular check-ups.

Limitations of Health Precautions Inferred from the Eight Characters

Bazi health analysis is only a probabilistic reference and cannot replace medical diagnosis. If health problems occur, one should first seek medical examination, relying primarily on scientific methods and using fortune-telling as a supplementary reference.
