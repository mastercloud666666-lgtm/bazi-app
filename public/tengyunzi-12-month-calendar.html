Before you subscribe

Clear answers about timing, delivery, and cancellation

Will I receive all twelve forecasts at once?

No. A new personal forecast is delivered at each BaZi solar-month change. This keeps the annual and monthly context current and gives you one useful planning brief at a time.

How is this different from the free Daily Almanac?

The free Daily Almanac is a general Chinese calendar and does not require a birth date. The paid forecast compares each solar month with your own natal chart and covers career, wealth, relationships, and wellbeing.

Can I subscribe if my birth hour is unknown?

Yes. Select Unknown when saving your profile. The forecast will use the available year, month, and day pillars and avoid claims that depend on the hour pillar.

Does the forecast guarantee an outcome?

No. It is a planning and self-knowledge tool. Use it alongside evidence, professional advice, resources, obligations, and your own judgment.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel automatic renewal from your Tengyunzi account. Your paid access continues until the end of the current billing period.
