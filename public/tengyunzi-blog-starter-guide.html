BaZi Starter Guide

New to BaZi? Start with the chart structure, learn the elements, find your Day Master, and only then move into timing and reports.

  • Beginner
  • Learning path
  • 12 min read

Step 1: Calculate Your Chart

Start with your birth date and hour if known. The free calculator gives you the four pillars and a basic structure to study.

Step 2: Find Your Day Master

The Day Master is the Heavenly Stem on the Day Pillar. It anchors the rest of the chart.

Step 3: Learn the Five Elements

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water describe balance, support, pressure, and expression. Do not rush past this layer.

Step 4: Add Timing

Luck Pillars, annual pillars, and monthly pillars explain why the same person can feel different in different seasons of life.
